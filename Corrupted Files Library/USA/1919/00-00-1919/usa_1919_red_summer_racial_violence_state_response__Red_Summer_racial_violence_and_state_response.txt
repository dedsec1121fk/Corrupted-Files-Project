Full story
----------
The Red Summer of 1919 saw racial violence across multiple U.S. cities and communities. The file matters because Black veterans, labor competition, white mobs, policing, press framing, and federal inaction combined into a national trust failure.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Racial violence erupted in multiple locations in 1919.
- Black communities often faced both mob violence and biased policing.
- The events are documented in press, NAACP, and historical records.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Local death counts and responsibility vary by city.
- Federal response must be read against the broader Red Scare climate.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- Some theories describe a coordinated national campaign of racial terror; coordination varied, but shared racist structures are well supported.
- The structural reading is that demobilized war power and white supremacy turned citizenship into contested terrain.
