Full story
----------
The Savings and Loan crisis showed how deregulation, risky lending, political pressure, deposit insurance, and fraud could create a public-cost disaster before the 2008 crisis. It is a bridge file for understanding moral hazard in U.S. finance.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Hundreds of thrifts failed during the crisis.
- The cleanup cost taxpayers large sums through federal resolution mechanisms.
- There were criminal prosecutions and major regulatory reforms.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Blame is split among deregulation, fraud, interest-rate shocks, weak supervision, and political influence.
- Not every failed institution was criminal.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says deregulation was designed to loot deposits and send the bill to taxpayers; some looting was real, but design claims need proof.
- A strong structural theory is privatized gain with insured public loss.

Source trail
------------
- Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation history
- Resolution Trust Corporation records
- Congressional hearings on S&L failures
