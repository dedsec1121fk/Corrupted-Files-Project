Iraq WMD intelligence failure
=============================

Country: USA
Year folder: 2003
Category: war / intelligence failure
Evidence level: documented intelligence and policy failure

Core story
----------
The US-led invasion of Iraq was justified partly through claims about weapons of mass
destruction that were not found as presented.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
This case is about how intelligence uncertainty, political pressure, defectors, media
repetition and post-9/11 fear produced a war narrative. The failure was not a minor
paperwork mistake; it led to invasion, occupation, mass casualties and regional
destabilization.

Timeline
--------
- 2002-2003: WMD claims dominate debate.
- 2003: invasion begins.
- Post-invasion: inspections fail to confirm central claims.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- No active WMD stockpiles were found as publicly argued.
- Prewar intelligence was heavily criticized.
- The war reshaped the Middle East and US trust.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It is one of the most important modern examples of evidence being overstated before war.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Debate continues over deliberate deception versus systemic failure; both readings point to
severe accountability problems.

-------------------------------------
