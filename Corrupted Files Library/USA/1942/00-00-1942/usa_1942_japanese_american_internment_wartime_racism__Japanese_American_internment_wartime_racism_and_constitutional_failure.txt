Full story
----------
After Pearl Harbor, the U.S. government forced Japanese Americans from the West Coast into camps. The case shows how fear, racism, military claims, and courts can combine to strip citizens and residents of liberty without individualized proof.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Executive Order 9066 enabled mass exclusion and detention.
- More than 100,000 people of Japanese ancestry were affected, many of them U.S. citizens.
- The U.S. later apologized and provided redress through the Civil Liberties Act of 1988.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Wartime intelligence claims were overstated and later discredited, but exact institutional motives vary by actor.
- The Supreme Court record remains a warning about judicial deference.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The theory lane claims internment was less security than racial land and labor removal; racist politics are documented, while local economic motives need separate proof.
- The structural reading is emergency power plus prejudice equals mass rights failure.

Source trail
------------
