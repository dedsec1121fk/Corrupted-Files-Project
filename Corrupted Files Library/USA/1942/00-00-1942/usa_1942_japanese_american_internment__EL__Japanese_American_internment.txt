Japanese American internment
============================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1942
Κατηγορία: wartime rights / mass detention
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented government injustice

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The US government forcibly removed and incarcerated Japanese Americans
during World War II.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Executive Order 9066 enabled mass removal from the West Coast. Families lost
homes, businesses and freedom without individualized evidence. Later government review
concluded the policy was driven by race prejudice, war hysteria and failure of political
leadership rather than military necessity.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1942: Executive Order 9066 signed.
- 1942-1945: incarceration camps operate.
- 1988: Civil Liberties Act apologizes and provides redress.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Over 100,000 people of Japanese ancestry were incarcerated.
- Many were US citizens.
- The US later issued apology and reparations.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: This is one of the clearest US examples of lawful paperwork producing unlawful
injustice.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: The wartime government claimed security necessity; later findings strongly rejected
that blanket justification.
