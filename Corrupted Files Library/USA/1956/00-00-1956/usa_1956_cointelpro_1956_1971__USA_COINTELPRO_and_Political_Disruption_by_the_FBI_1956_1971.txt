USA: COINTELPRO and Political Disruption by the FBI (1956-1971)

1) Infiltration, disruption, and covert manipulation against political targets
Status: [DOCUMENTED]

Summary:
COINTELPRO was the FBI’s Counterintelligence Program. The FBI says it began in 1956 against the Communist Party USA and later expanded to other targets. The Church Committee and later disclosures showed the program went far beyond passive observation. It used infiltrators, forged letters, rumor campaigns, pressure on employers and institutions, and other tactics aimed at disruption, discrediting, fragmentation, and political neutralization.

Why it matters:
COINTELPRO is one of the strongest documented answers to the claim that Western democracies would never run covert political operations against their own population. They did.

What is proved:
- The FBI formally ran COINTELPRO.
- The program targeted domestic political organizations.
- The Church Committee identified broad intelligence abuses across federal agencies, with COINTELPRO as a major example.

What remains debated:
- The exact full extent of damage in every individual case is still debated.
- Popular summaries often compress different target groups and time periods together.

When people say “they infiltrate and destabilize groups from inside,” COINTELPRO is one of the clearest documented cases.
