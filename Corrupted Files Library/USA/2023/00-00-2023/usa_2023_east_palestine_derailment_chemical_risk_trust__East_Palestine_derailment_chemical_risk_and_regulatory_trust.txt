Full story
----------
The East Palestine derailment in Ohio became a public-trust crisis about rail safety, hazardous cargo, controlled burns, evacuation, environmental monitoring, corporate responsibility, and whether residents could trust official reassurance after chemical exposure fears.

Confirmed record
----------------
- A Norfolk Southern train derailed in East Palestine in 2023.
- Hazardous materials and a controlled burn became central public concerns.
- Federal and state agencies monitored environmental and health questions.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------

- Political narratives sometimes outpaced technical evidence.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane

- A structural reading is that deregulated freight systems externalize risk onto small towns.
