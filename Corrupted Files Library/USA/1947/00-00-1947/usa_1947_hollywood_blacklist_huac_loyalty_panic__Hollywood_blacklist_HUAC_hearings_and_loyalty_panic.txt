Country: USA

Full story
----------
The Hollywood blacklist turned Cold War fear into employment punishment, self-censorship, informant pressure, and cultural control. The file shows how public hearings and private studio decisions can work together without needing one formal censorship ministry.

Confirmed record
----------------
- HUAC hearings targeted alleged communist influence in Hollywood.
- The Hollywood Ten were cited for contempt and blacklisting spread through the industry.
- Many careers were damaged or forced underground.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Some individuals had left-wing or communist ties; the public-trust issue is punishment without fair individual proof of wrongdoing.
- Private studio choices complicate simple state-censorship labels.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says a cultural purge was coordinated to discipline all dissent; coordination existed through hearings, studios, and pressure, but each firing has its own record.
- A structural reading is fear outsourcing: government pressure made private employers police ideology.
