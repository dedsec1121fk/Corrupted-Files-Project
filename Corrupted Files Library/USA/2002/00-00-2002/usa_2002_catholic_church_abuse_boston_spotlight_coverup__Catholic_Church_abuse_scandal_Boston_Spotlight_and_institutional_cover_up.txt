Full story
----------
The Boston clergy-abuse revelations showed that institutional reputation, hierarchy, secrecy, legal settlements, and victim disbelief can protect offenders for years. The case is a core public-trust file because moral authority became a shield against accountability.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Investigative reporting and court files exposed patterns of abuse and reassignment.
- The scandal led to resignations, settlements, and wider investigations across dioceses.
- Victim testimony became central to public understanding.
