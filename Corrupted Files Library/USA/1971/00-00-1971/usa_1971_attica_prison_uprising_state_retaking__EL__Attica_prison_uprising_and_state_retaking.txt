Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Attica began from prison conditions, racial tension and demands for dignity.
The retaking became a bloodbath. Official narratives initially blamed prisoners for deaths
that ballistics and later investigations contradicted. The file is a textbook example of how
the first official story after violence can be wrong and how corrections may come too late.
