Core story
----------
New York authorities retook Attica prison after an uprising, leaving prisoners and hostages
dead.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
Attica began from prison conditions, racial tension and demands for dignity. The retaking
became a bloodbath. Official narratives initially blamed prisoners for deaths that
ballistics and later investigations contradicted. The file is a textbook example of how the
first official story after violence can be wrong and how corrections may come too late.

Timeline
--------
- 1971: uprising begins.
- September 1971: state retakes prison.
- Aftermath: investigations and lawsuits continue for decades.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Dozens died during the retaking.
- Initial claims about prisoner killings of hostages were disproven.
- Attica shaped prison reform and distrust.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It matters because prisons are places where public visibility is low and state power is
high.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
The state argued it faced a dangerous hostage crisis. Critics emphasize excessive force,
misinformation and lack of accountability.
