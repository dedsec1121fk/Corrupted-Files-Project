Full story
----------
The Pentagon Papers revealed that U.S. leaders had privately understood more about Vietnam's failures and escalation risks than they admitted publicly. The case became a landmark of press freedom because the government tried to stop publication while claiming national security.

Confirmed record
----------------
- The leaked Defense Department history documented internal decision-making on Vietnam.
- The New York Times and Washington Post litigation reached the Supreme Court.
- The case strengthened public understanding of the gap between official statements and internal records.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- The papers did not prove every antiwar claim, but they did document patterns of misleading public presentation.
- National-security harm claims remain historically debated.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says all war explanations are manufactured; the documented lesson is narrower and stronger: governments can hide internal doubt while selling certainty.
- A structural theory is classification abuse: secrecy protects policy reputation as much as genuine operations.
