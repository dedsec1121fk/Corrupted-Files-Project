Wounded Knee Massacre
=====================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1890
Κατηγορία: state violence / indigenous history
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented historical atrocity

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Federal troops killed Lakota people near Wounded Knee Creek after years of
dispossession and coercive policy.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The event is often described as a battle in older official language, but
historical work and Native testimony treat it as a massacre rooted in broken treaties,
forced reservation life, fear of the Ghost Dance movement, and military pressure. The file
matters because it shows how state narratives can rename violence and how the archive can
preserve official language while communities preserve trauma.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1890: Seventh Cavalry confronts Lakota near Wounded Knee.
- After a shot or struggle, soldiers fire heavily into the camp.
- Later decades: Native activists and historians challenge the official battle framing.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Hundreds of Lakota men, women and children died; exact numbers vary by source.
- The event followed US suppression of the Ghost Dance movement.
- Medals awarded to soldiers remain a major controversy.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: Wounded Knee is a foundation case for understanding how law, military force, racial
ideology, and land policy can merge into a single system.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: The basic killing is not in doubt. Interpretations differ over immediate triggers,
but the deeper context of coercive policy is central.
