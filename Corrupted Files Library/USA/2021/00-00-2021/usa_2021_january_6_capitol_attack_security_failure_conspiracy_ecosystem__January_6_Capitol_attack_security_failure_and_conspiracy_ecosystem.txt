Full story
----------
The January 6 attack on the U.S. Capitol was a public-trust rupture: election-fraud claims, online mobilization, extremist networks, presidential rhetoric, policing failures, and congressional certification collided in a live assault on constitutional transfer of power.

Confirmed record
----------------
- The Capitol was breached on January 6, 2021, during certification of the 2020 election.
- Hundreds of criminal prosecutions followed.
- Congressional and inspector-general reviews examined security and intelligence failures.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Political responsibility and intent are debated across legal, congressional, and partisan forums.
- Claims of undercover orchestration require evidence distinct from documented extremist and protester actions.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy ecosystem includes stolen-election claims and false-flag claims; both should be tested against court records, communications, video, and charging documents.
- The structural theory is that repeated legitimacy lies can mobilize real violence even when individual believers think they defend democracy.
