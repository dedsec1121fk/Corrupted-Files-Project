Core story
----------
The 2000 US presidential election was effectively decided after recount litigation reached
the Supreme Court.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The case shows how ballots, machines, deadlines, courts and legitimacy all meet under
pressure. Florida’s recount chaos became a national trauma. The Supreme Court decision ended
the recount, awarding the presidency in practical terms. The corruption question is not
simply whether votes were stolen; it is whether systems were too fragile, partisan and
legally inconsistent to produce trust.

Timeline
--------
- Nov 2000: contested Florida result.
- Dec 2000: Supreme Court decision.
- Aftermath: legitimacy debate continues.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Florida vote margin was extremely narrow.
- Supreme Court stopped the recount.
- Election administration reform followed.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It remains a core case for election infrastructure and judicial legitimacy.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Partisans still interpret the outcome differently; the procedural chaos itself is
documented.
