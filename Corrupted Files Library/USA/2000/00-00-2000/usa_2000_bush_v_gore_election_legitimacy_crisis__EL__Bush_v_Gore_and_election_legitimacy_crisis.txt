Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case shows how ballots, machines, deadlines, courts and legitimacy all
meet under pressure. Florida’s recount chaos became a national trauma. The Supreme Court
decision ended the recount, awarding the presidency in practical terms. The corruption
question is not simply whether votes were stolen; it is whether systems were too fragile,
partisan and legally inconsistent to produce trust.
