Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: A videotaped beating by Los Angeles police officers exposed police violence
to a national audience.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Rodney King case became a turning point because video pierced official
narration. The public saw force that could not be easily reduced to paperwork. The
acquittals in state court triggered the 1992 Los Angeles uprising, showing how one case can
detonate years of accumulated grievance over policing, race and inequality.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1991: beating recorded.
- 1992: state verdicts trigger uprising.
- 1993: federal convictions.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Video evidence circulated nationally.
- State acquittals sparked unrest.
- Federal civil-rights convictions followed for some officers.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It shows why visual evidence changes power but does not automatically create
justice.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Some defended the officers through compliance narratives; the video and later
federal case kept the abuse central.
