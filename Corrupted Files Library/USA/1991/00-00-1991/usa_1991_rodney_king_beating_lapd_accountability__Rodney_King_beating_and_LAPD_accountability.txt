===========================================

Country: USA

Core story
----------
A videotaped beating by Los Angeles police officers exposed police violence to a national
audience.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The Rodney King case became a turning point because video pierced official narration. The
public saw force that could not be easily reduced to paperwork. The acquittals in state
court triggered the 1992 Los Angeles uprising, showing how one case can detonate years of
accumulated grievance over policing, race and inequality.

Timeline
--------
- 1991: beating recorded.
- 1992: state verdicts trigger uprising.
- 1993: federal convictions.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Video evidence circulated nationally.
- State acquittals sparked unrest.
- Federal civil-rights convictions followed for some officers.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It shows why visual evidence changes power but does not automatically create justice.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Some defended the officers through compliance narratives; the video and later federal case
kept the abuse central.
