Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Striking coal miners and their families in Colorado were attacked during a
labor conflict involving company guards and the National Guard.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Ludlow case shows how a labor dispute became a battlefield. Workers were
fighting for pay, safety, union recognition and dignity. Company power, private guards,
state militia and public order rhetoric converged. The deaths of women and children in a
tent colony made Ludlow one of the darkest symbols of American labor history.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1913-1914: Colorado coal strike escalates.
- April 1914: Violence at Ludlow tent colony.
- Aftermath: national outrage and investigations.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Tent colony families were among the victims.
- The conflict involved Colorado Fuel and Iron interests.
- Public backlash helped push later labor reform debates.
