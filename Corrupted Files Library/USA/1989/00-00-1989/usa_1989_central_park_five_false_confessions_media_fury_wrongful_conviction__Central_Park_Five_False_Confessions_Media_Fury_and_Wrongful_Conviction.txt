1. Why this file exists
The Central Park Five case shows how police pressure, media rage and racialized fear can harden into convictions even when the evidence does not truly hold.

2. Background and setting
Five teenagers were convicted in the attack on a jogger in Central Park. Years later, another man confessed and DNA evidence supported his confession; the convictions were vacated. The case remains a warning about interrogation, youth vulnerability, headlines and political appetite for punishment.

3. Chronology
- 1989: attack and arrests dominate New York media.
- 1990: convictions follow.
- 2002: convictions are vacated after confession and DNA review.
- 2014: New York City settles civil claims.

4. What happened in practical terms
The mechanism was narrative capture. Once the young men were portrayed as monsters, inconsistencies became secondary. Confessions extracted under pressure were treated as stronger than physical contradictions.

5. Evidence map
Documented elements:
- The convictions were vacated.
- DNA evidence supported another perpetrator.
- The case is widely used in wrongful-conviction education.

Alleged or disputed elements:
- Debates continue over every police decision and prosecutor judgment.
- The city settlement did not mean every actor admitted wrongdoing.

Unsupported or overextended claims to treat carefully:
- Claims that no crime occurred are false; the crime occurred, but the convicted youths were not the perpetrators under the later official conclusion.

6. Human impact
The boys lost years of life, safety, reputation and childhood. Their families carried stigma long after headlines moved on.

7. Why the case created suspicion
Suspicion grew because the case exposed how quickly public anger can become evidence in the court of public opinion.

8. Counterpoints and caution
A counterpoint is that investigators faced a horrific crime and intense pressure. That pressure explains decisions; it does not excuse false certainty.

9. What a reader should conclude
The case proves that a justice system can be most dangerous when it feels most certain.
