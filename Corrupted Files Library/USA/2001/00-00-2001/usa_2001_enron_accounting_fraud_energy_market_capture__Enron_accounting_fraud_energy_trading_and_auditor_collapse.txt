Full story
----------
Enron collapsed after hidden debt, accounting tricks, energy-market manipulation claims, and auditor failure broke the company's image as an innovation miracle. It became a public-trust case about whether complexity can be used as camouflage for looting.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Executives were prosecuted and convicted after the collapse.
- Arthur Andersen's auditing role became a symbol of watchdog failure.
- Employees and investors suffered large losses while insiders had better information.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- The full boundary between aggressive accounting, deception, and regulatory failure differs by transaction.
- California energy-market manipulation allegations add a separate but related layer.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- Some theories frame Enron as proof that all markets are scripted by insiders; the documented record proves a major fraud, not universal market fakery.
- A stronger structural theory is that deregulation, political access, and audit conflicts let deception scale.

Why it matters
--------------
It matters because technical finance can hide simple theft behind vocabulary that ordinary investors cannot audit.
