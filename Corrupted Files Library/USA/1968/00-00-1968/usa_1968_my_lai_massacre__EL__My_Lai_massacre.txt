My Lai massacre
===============

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1968
Κατηγορία: war crimes / military accountability
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented war crime

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: US soldiers killed Vietnamese civilians in My Lai; the cover-up and limited
accountability became a defining Vietnam War scandal.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: My Lai was not only a battlefield atrocity. It was also a chain-of-command
and institutional truth case. Civilians were killed, reports were suppressed or minimized,
and the public learned how military systems can protect reputation before victims. The
eventual exposure showed the importance of whistleblowers, journalists and congressional
scrutiny.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1968: massacre occurs.
- 1969: public learns of the atrocity.
- 1970s: trials and public debate.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Hundreds of civilians were killed.
- The massacre was initially concealed.
- Only limited criminal accountability followed.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is a core case for studying war crimes, dehumanization and command
responsibility.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: The killings are documented. Debate continues over how widespread similar conduct
was and how responsibility should have been assigned.
