Core story
----------
The Supreme Court decision changed campaign finance by expanding independent political
spending rights.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
Citizens United did not create money in politics from nothing, but it changed the legal
terrain. Super PACs and dark-money networks grew in influence, making it harder for ordinary
voters to trace who funds persuasion. The case is not a secret conspiracy; it is a public
legal transformation with deep democratic effects.

Timeline
--------
- 2010: Supreme Court decision.
- 2010s: Super PAC and dark-money ecosystem grows.
- Ongoing: reform debates continue.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Decision protected independent expenditures.
- Political spending vehicles expanded afterward.
- Disclosure gaps remain a central issue.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It belongs in the database because visible law can still create hidden power.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Supporters frame the ruling as speech protection; critics see donor power and disclosure
failure.
