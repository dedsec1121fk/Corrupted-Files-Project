Full story
----------
The Pullman Strike showed how corporate wage decisions, company-town control, rail networks, injunctions, and federal troops could turn a labor dispute into a national confrontation over whose order the state protects.

Confirmed record
----------------
- The 1894 strike disrupted rail traffic and mail movement.
- Federal injunctions and troops were used against the strike.
- The conflict became a landmark in U.S. labor and state-power history.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Supporters emphasized public order and mail; critics emphasized corporate-state alignment.
- The legality of intervention and injunction strategy remains historically debated.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says the state was openly captured by rail capital; the record strongly shows alignment, while capture should be argued through decisions and actors.
- A structural reading is that infrastructure dependency gave corporate disputes national-security language.
