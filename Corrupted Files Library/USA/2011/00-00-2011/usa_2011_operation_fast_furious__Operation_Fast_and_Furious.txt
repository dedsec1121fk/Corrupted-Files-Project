Operation Fast and Furious
==========================

Country: USA
Year folder: 2011
Category: law enforcement failure
Evidence level: documented oversight scandal

Core story
----------
A firearms trafficking investigation allowed guns to move into criminal networks, causing a
major federal scandal.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
Fast and Furious was designed to trace trafficking networks, but the operation became a
warning about controlled-risk strategies that lose control. When guns connected to the
operation were linked to violence, the scandal became a symbol of bureaucratic failure,
political blame and weak oversight.

Timeline
--------
- 2009-2011: operation and fallout.
- 2011: public controversy intensifies.
- Aftermath: oversight and internal reviews.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- ATF operation allowed firearms purchases to proceed.
- Weapons were later linked to crimes.
- Congressional investigations and contempt fight followed.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It shows how law enforcement can create the danger it claims to map if controls fail.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Partisans used the case heavily; the core operational failure is still documented.

Source trail / suggested verification
-------------------------------------
