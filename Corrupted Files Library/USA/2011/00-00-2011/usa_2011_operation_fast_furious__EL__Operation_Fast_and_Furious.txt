Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: A firearms trafficking investigation allowed guns to move into criminal
networks, causing a major federal scandal.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Fast and Furious was designed to trace trafficking networks, but the
operation became a warning about controlled-risk strategies that lose control. When guns
connected to the operation were linked to violence, the scandal became a symbol of
bureaucratic failure, political blame and weak oversight.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2009-2011: operation and fallout.
- 2011: public controversy intensifies.
- Aftermath: oversight and internal reviews.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- ATF operation allowed firearms purchases to proceed.
- Weapons were later linked to crimes.
- Congressional investigations and contempt fight followed.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It shows how law enforcement can create the danger it claims to map if controls
fail.
