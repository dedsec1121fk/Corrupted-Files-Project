Full story
----------
The 1998 Tobacco Master Settlement Agreement followed decades of public-health conflict over smoking, addiction, advertising, and corporate knowledge. It is a case about how industries can shape science, politics, and liability while harm accumulates slowly.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Major tobacco companies settled with U.S. states in 1998.
- Documents and litigation revealed industry knowledge and strategies.
- The settlement changed advertising rules and state revenue streams.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Whether settlement money was used for public health as promised varies by state.
- Individual responsibility and corporate deception are often argued against each other.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says the industry bought decades of political silence; lobbying and deception are documented, but every silence claim needs evidence.
- The structural reading is regulatory delay: slow harms allow profit before accountability catches up.
