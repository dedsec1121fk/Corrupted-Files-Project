Core story
----------
US officials secretly facilitated arms sales to Iran and diverted proceeds to Contra rebels
despite legal restrictions.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
Iran-Contra exposed a shadow foreign policy: classified networks, deniable actors, money
channels and ideological goals operating around congressional limits. It showed how
executive power can treat the law as an obstacle to route around, not a boundary.

Timeline
--------
- 1986: scandal becomes public.
- 1987: congressional hearings.
- 1990s: pardons close many legal consequences.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Arms sales to Iran contradicted public policy.
- Funds supported Contra rebels in Nicaragua.
- Hearings and prosecutions followed, with later pardons.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It is a classic file about secrecy, deniability and constitutional limits.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Some participants framed actions as anti-communist necessity or hostage diplomacy. The
scandal remains documented as a serious breach of law and accountability.
