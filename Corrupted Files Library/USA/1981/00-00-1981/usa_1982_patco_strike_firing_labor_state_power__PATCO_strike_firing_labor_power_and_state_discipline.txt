Full story
----------
When the Reagan administration fired striking air-traffic controllers, the PATCO case became a signal event in U.S. labor history. It showed how public safety law, executive power, and anti-union politics could reshape bargaining expectations far beyond one union.

Confirmed record
----------------
- PATCO members struck in 1981 despite legal restrictions.
- The federal government fired many controllers and decertified the union.
- The event became a symbol of a harder line against organized labor.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Public safety and labor rights claims collided.
- The exact causal role in long-term union decline is debated.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says the strike was used as a planned signal to break labor nationwide; intent is debated, but the signal effect is widely recognized.
- The structural reading is that state discipline can reset private-sector expectations.
