Full story
----------
In 1979, Ku Klux Klan and Nazi members attacked an anti-Klan demonstration in Greensboro, North Carolina, killing five people. The case remains a public-trust file because informants, police absence, acquittals, and later civil findings kept the question of state responsibility alive.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Five demonstrators were killed in Greensboro in 1979.
- Criminal trials ended in acquittals.
- Civil litigation later found liability involving some attackers and police-related failures.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
