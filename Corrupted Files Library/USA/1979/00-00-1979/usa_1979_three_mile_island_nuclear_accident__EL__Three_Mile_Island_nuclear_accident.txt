Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: A partial nuclear meltdown in Pennsylvania revealed how technical failures,
human error and confused communication can magnify public fear.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Three Mile Island did not produce the mass casualty scenario feared by some,
but it permanently changed nuclear politics. The crisis was also informational: officials,
operators, journalists and citizens struggled to understand what was happening in real time.
It shows how complex infrastructure requires transparent communication as much as
engineering.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1979: accident unfolds.
- Afterward: cleanup and investigations.
- Long-term: nuclear expansion faces stronger resistance.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Partial meltdown occurred at Unit 2.
- Public communication failures increased fear.
- Nuclear regulation and industry practices changed.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
