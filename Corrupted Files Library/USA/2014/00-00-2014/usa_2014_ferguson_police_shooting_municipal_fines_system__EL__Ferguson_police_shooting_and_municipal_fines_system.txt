Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The killing of Michael Brown triggered protests and a DOJ investigation
into Ferguson’s police and municipal court practices.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------

system using fines, fees and enforcement in ways that burdened Black residents and funded
government priorities. This turned a police case into a governance case: public safety was
entangled with revenue extraction.
