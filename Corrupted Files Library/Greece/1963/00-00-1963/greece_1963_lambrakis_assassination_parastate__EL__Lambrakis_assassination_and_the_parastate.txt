Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The killing of MP Grigoris Lambrakis exposed links between far-right
violence, police tolerance and a deeper parastate.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Lambrakis was not just a murder victim; he became evidence of a shadow
system. The investigation suggested that official democracy had unofficial enforcers. The
case helped prepare public understanding of the later dictatorship because it showed that
anti-democratic networks already existed beneath formal institutions.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1963: Lambrakis attacked and dies.
- Investigation exposes parastate links.
- 1967: dictatorship confirms fears of anti-democratic networks.
