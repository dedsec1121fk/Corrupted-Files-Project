Core story
----------
Makronisos became a symbol of postwar political persecution, forced declarations and re-
education.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The camp system aimed not merely to imprison but to break and remake political identity.
Detainees were pressured to renounce beliefs, sign statements and return to society as
examples of national correction. The file belongs here because it shows psychology used as
state discipline before modern digital control existed.

Key facts and evidence points

- Declarations of repentance were central.
- The island remains a symbol of state repression.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It connects civil-war memory, political policing and the psychology of forced loyalty.

Competing explanations and caution

and coercion.
