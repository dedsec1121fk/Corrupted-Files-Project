Metaxas dictatorship and censorship machine
===========================================

Country: Greece
Year folder: 1936
Category: authoritarianism / censorship
Evidence level: documented dictatorship

Core story
----------
The 4th of August regime suspended democratic life, censored culture and promoted an
authoritarian national project.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The regime used anti-communism, royal backing, youth mobilization, censorship and police
files to build obedience. Its importance for this database is that authoritarianism did not
arrive only through foreign occupation; it grew from internal crisis, fear and elite
acceptance.

Timeline
--------
- 1936: dictatorship established.
- 1936-1941: censorship and repression.
- 1940: war with Italy changes memory of regime.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Political parties were suppressed.
- Censorship and exile were used.
- The regime built a youth organization and state ideology.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It helps readers recognize how fear of disorder can justify anti-democratic control.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Some remember Metaxas through the later Ohi moment; that does not erase dictatorship and
repression before the war.

Source trail / suggested verification
-------------------------------------
- US National Archives: https://www.archives.gov/
