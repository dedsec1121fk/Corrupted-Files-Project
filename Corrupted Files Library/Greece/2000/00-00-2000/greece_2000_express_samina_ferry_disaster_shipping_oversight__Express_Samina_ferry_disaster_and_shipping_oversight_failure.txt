Full story
----------
The Express Samina sank near Paros in 2000, killing dozens of passengers. The case became more than a navigation failure because it exposed questions about crew conduct, ship condition, regulatory oversight, emergency response, and whether ferry safety had been treated as routine paperwork.

Confirmed record
----------------
- The ship struck rocks and sank near Paros.
- Eighty people died according to the public record.
- Trials and regulatory scrutiny followed.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Legal blame focused on individuals, but systemic safety culture remains the larger question.
- The degree to which commercial pressure shaped safety practice is harder to document.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- Rumors about protected shipping interests should be treated carefully; the documented core is regulatory weakness and operational failure.
- The structural theory is that island lifelines can become dangerous when market necessity normalizes old equipment and weak inspection.
