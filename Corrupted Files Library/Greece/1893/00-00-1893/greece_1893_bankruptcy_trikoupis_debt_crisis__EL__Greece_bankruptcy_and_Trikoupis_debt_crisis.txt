Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Greece’s 1893 bankruptcy became a foundational memory of debt, foreign
creditors and political limits.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case matters because Greek public life repeatedly returns to the same
triangle: borrowing, modernization promises and external supervision. Trikoupis did not
simply announce a number; he announced a national limitation. Later generations remembered
the phrase as evidence that economics can become a sovereignty crisis.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1893: state insolvency announced.
- 1898: International Financial Control imposed after war defeat.
- Long-term: debt sovereignty becomes recurring Greek theme.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Debt pressure limited state choices.
- Foreign financial control later shaped Greek policy.
- The event became a phrase in Greek political memory.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It helps readers connect older Greek debt cycles with later bailouts without
pretending history repeats mechanically.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: The bankruptcy had structural causes, political choices and international
constraints. Blaming one man or one faction is too simple.
