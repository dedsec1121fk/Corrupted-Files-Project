Full story
----------
The 2024 email leak controversy around Greek expatriate voters and postal-vote communication became a trust case because election data is not ordinary marketing data. Citizens asked how political actors obtained contact information and whether state-held democratic data could be treated as party infrastructure.

Confirmed record
----------------
- The controversy involved emails sent to Greeks abroad and official data-protection scrutiny.
- Resignations and political accountability steps followed.
- The case overlapped with rollout of postal-vote systems and trust in electoral administration.
