GREECE: Personal Number, Biometric IDs, and the State Digital Layer (2022-2026)

Status: [DOCUMENTED] for the rollout of gov.gr Wallet, the new Personal Number system, biometric identity infrastructure, and wider state digitization.
Status: [ALLEGED / OVERSTATED] for claims that these tools already amount to a complete social-credit regime.

Summary:
Greece has been building a deeper digital identity layer:
- gov.gr Wallet allows digital storage and verification of official documents such as ID cards and driving licences.
- New identity-document processes and related systems rely on biometric features.
- The Personal Number system consolidates multiple citizen identifiers into a more unified state-facing number and is linked to broader e-government workflows.
- These tools are usually framed as convenience and anti-bureaucracy measures, but they also raise privacy, aggregation, and misuse concerns.

Why it matters:
This file is not about saying "digital identity = dictatorship." It is about documenting how administrative efficiency, once centralized and linked across systems, can also expand the state's practical ability to verify, track, gate, and authenticate.

What this archive does NOT claim:
- It does NOT claim Greece already runs a full social-credit system.
- It does NOT claim every digital-ID tool is inherently malicious.
- It does claim that digital identity, biometrics, and state databases reduce friction for both service delivery and control.
