Country: Greece
Year: 1974

Full story
----------
The Greek junta backed the coup against Makarios in Cyprus, triggering a chain that preceded Turkey's invasion and the island's partition. The file belongs here because it is a state-failure disaster where military rule, nationalism, foreign alliances, and secrecy converged.

Confirmed record
----------------
- The Athens junta's role in the coup is part of the historical record.
- Turkey invaded Cyprus in 1974 after the coup, creating long-term occupation and displacement.
- The disaster contributed to the fall of the Greek dictatorship.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Responsibility is distributed across Greek junta actors, Cypriot factions, Turkish planning, NATO pressures, and U.S./British diplomacy.
- Many archival questions remain politically sensitive.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy version claims the island was knowingly sacrificed by great powers; declassified diplomacy shows cold strategic calculations, but every strong claim needs document support.
- The structural reading is that authoritarian secrecy made catastrophic foreign-policy gambling possible.
