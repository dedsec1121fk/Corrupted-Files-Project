Country: Greece
Year: 2015
Category: migration / EU policy / humanitarian governance

Full story
----------
The 2015 refugee crisis placed Greek islands at the center of European border politics. Boats, deaths, volunteers, asylum delays, EU bargaining, Turkey relations, and local pressure formed a system where humanitarian language often collided with deterrence.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Large numbers of refugees and migrants crossed the Aegean in 2015.
- Greek islands became key registration and containment sites.
- The EU-Turkey framework reshaped movement and asylum pressure.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Responsibility is split among Greek authorities, EU institutions, Turkey, smugglers, and war conditions.
- Security, humanitarian, and local-capacity claims often compete.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- Some theories claim migration was engineered as a weapon against Greece or Europe; state exploitation of flows can occur, but broad replacement plots are not evidence.
- The structural reading is deterrence by suffering: harsh conditions can become policy even when nobody says so plainly.
