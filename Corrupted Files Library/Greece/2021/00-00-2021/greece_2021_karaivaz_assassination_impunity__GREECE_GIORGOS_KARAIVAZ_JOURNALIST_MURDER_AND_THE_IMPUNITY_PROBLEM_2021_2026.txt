Classification: [STRONG EVIDENCE]

Why this file exists:
When a crime reporter is murdered and nobody is ultimately held responsible, the issue becomes larger than one case. It becomes a test of whether organized crime, political influence, and state weakness overlap.

What is documented:
- Reuters reported that Karaivaz, a veteran crime journalist, was shot dead near his Athens home in April 2021 in what looked like a professional hit.
- Reuters reported in July 2024 that the two men charged over the killing were acquitted for lack of evidence.
- CPJ reported in 2025 that an Athens court ruled in December 2024 that Karaivaz was murdered because of his journalistic work, while still leaving the killing unsolved.
- CPJ said total impunity persists and that nobody has been held responsible for the murder.

Why it matters:
- This is not only about one murder. It is about the message sent to reporters covering crime, police links, and corruption.
- An unsolved contract-style killing of a journalist becomes a symbol of state weakness even if the state did not order the crime.

What is NOT proved:
- The public record does not prove who ordered the killing.
- It does support three strong conclusions: the killing looked professional, the journalistic-work motive has been judicially recognized, and impunity remains.

Logical reading:
- Even without proving a state conspiracy, the case fits a darker pattern: when violence hits reporters and accountability collapses, democratic institutions look weaker than the networks they are supposed to contain.
