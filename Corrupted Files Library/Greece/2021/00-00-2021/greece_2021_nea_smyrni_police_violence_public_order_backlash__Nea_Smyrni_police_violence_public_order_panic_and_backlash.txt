Full story
----------
The Nea Smyrni incidents during pandemic restrictions became a flashpoint for Greek distrust of public-order policing. Video of police violence, mass protest, later clashes, and injury to an officer created competing narratives about abuse, lawlessness, and escalation.

Confirmed record
----------------
- A police violence video triggered public outrage.
- Large protests and clashes followed.
- The case became part of a wider debate over policing during COVID-era restrictions.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Different sides emphasize initial police violence or later protest violence.
- The proportionality of pandemic public-order enforcement remains contested.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says authorities deliberately provoked escalation to justify repression; evidence should be incident-specific.
- The structural reading is that stressed emergency rules made routine policing more explosive.
