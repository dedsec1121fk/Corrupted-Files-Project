Core story
----------
Occupation Greece suffered famine, black market profiteering, resistance, collaboration and
mass trauma.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The famine was not only natural scarcity. It involved occupation extraction, transport
collapse, blockade conditions, black-market networks and state breakdown. In this
environment, some people resisted, some survived through compromise, and some profited. The
case shows how social morality changes under hunger.

Timeline
--------
- 1941: occupation begins.
- 1941-1942: famine crisis.
- 1944: liberation leaves unresolved divisions.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Famine killed large numbers, especially in urban areas.
- Collaborationist structures existed under occupation.
- Resistance and reprisal cycles intensified violence.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It helps explain postwar bitterness, civil conflict and distrust toward elites who survived
comfortably.

Competing explanations and caution
