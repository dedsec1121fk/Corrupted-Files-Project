Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The police killing of Alexandros Grigoropoulos triggered nationwide unrest
and became a generational rupture.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The shooting converted accumulated youth anger, economic insecurity and
police distrust into street revolt. It was not only about one officer; it was about whether
young people believed the state saw them as citizens or enemies.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- Dec 2008: shooting.
- Dec 2008: nationwide unrest.
- Afterward: trials and long-term political memory.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Grigoropoulos was 15 years old.
- The killing occurred in Exarchia.
- Mass protests and riots followed across Greece.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is a central modern Greek file on policing, youth rage and legitimacy.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
