=========================================

Core story
----------
The police killing of Alexandros Grigoropoulos triggered nationwide unrest and became a
generational rupture.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The shooting converted accumulated youth anger, economic insecurity and police distrust into
street revolt. It was not only about one officer; it was about whether young people believed
the state saw them as citizens or enemies.

Timeline
--------
- Dec 2008: shooting.
- Dec 2008: nationwide unrest.
- Afterward: trials and long-term political memory.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Grigoropoulos was 15 years old.
- The killing occurred in Exarchia.
- Mass protests and riots followed across Greece.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It is a central modern Greek file on policing, youth rage and legitimacy.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Some debate the scale and tactics of riots; the killing and its symbolic force are
documented.
