How the blood relation is usually described:
- Eleftherios Venizelos' sister, Katingo (Aikaterini) Venizelou, married into the Mitsotakis family.
- Konstantinos Mitsotakis is commonly described in mainstream biographies and reporting as Venizelos' nephew or close family descendant through that line.
- Kyriakos Mitsotakis is then described in current reporting as Venizelos' great-uncle / great-great-nephew link depending on the exact family-tree phrasing used by the source.

Why this matters beyond genealogy:
The point is not gossip. The point is that Greek public life has repeatedly recycled major surnames: Venizelos, Papandreou, Karamanlis, Mitsotakis. That does not automatically prove corruption in any individual case, but it does show how political access, recognition, networks, and legitimacy can stay concentrated over generations.

What is proved:
- The Mitsotakis-Venizelos family connection is real and openly acknowledged in mainstream reporting.
- Greece has a long-documented pattern of dynastic politics.

What should be stated carefully:
