USA — PROJECT BLUE BOOK AND UAP: ARCHIVES, ALIENS, AND PUBLIC TRUST (1947–2026) EVIDENCE STATUS: [DOCUMENTED] Project BLUE BOOK archive; [UNPROVEN] extraterrestrial interpretation. ARTICLE Project BLUE BOOK is the bridge between documented state files and the modern UFO/UAP imagination. The U.S. Air Force investigated thousands of UFO reports from 1947 to 1969. The National Archives says the project is declassified and available for research; it also repeats the Air Force figure of 12,618 sightings, of which 701 remained unidentified. The Air Force conclusion was that the investigated reports did not show a national-security threat, did not prove science beyond known knowledge, and did not prove extraterrestrial vehicles.
