Greensboro massacre, police absence, and extremist violence — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Five demonstrators were killed in Greensboro in 1979.
- Criminal trials ended in acquittals.
- Civil litigation later found liability involving some attackers and police-related failures.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- The exact degree of police knowledge and preventability remains debated.
- Informant roles require careful source handling.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says police knowingly allowed a political assassination; the record supports serious questions, but intent must be tied to documents and testimony.
- The structural reading is that extremist violence becomes more dangerous when law enforcement treats targets as politically marginal.
