Three Mile Island nuclear accident ================================== Country: USA Year folder: 1979 Category: industrial risk / regulation Evidence level: documented accident Core story ---------- A partial nuclear meltdown in Pennsylvania revealed how technical failures, human error and confused communication can magnify public fear. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Three Mile Island did not produce the mass casualty scenario feared by some, but it permanently changed nuclear politics. The crisis was also informational: officials, operators, journalists and citizens struggled to understand what was happening in real time. It shows how complex infrastructure requires transparent communication as much as engineering.
