FBI surveillance of Martin Luther King Jr. and political blackmail (1960s) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 1965 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Surveillance, secrecy and information control FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER FBI surveillance moved from security into political weaponry when intimate information was used to pressure and discredit a civil-rights leader. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is secrecy plus asymmetry: one side collects, classifies, stores or shapes information while ordinary people cannot see the full system. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
