Fast and Furious, ATF gunwalking, oversight and border violence (2009-2012) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 2011 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Surveillance, secrecy and information control FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER A gun-trafficking investigation lost control of weapons and exposed operational hubris and oversight failure. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is secrecy plus asymmetry: one side collects, classifies, stores or shapes information while ordinary people cannot see the full system. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
