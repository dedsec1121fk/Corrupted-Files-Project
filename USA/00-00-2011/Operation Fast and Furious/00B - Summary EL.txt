Operation Fast and Furious ========================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2011 Κατηγορία: law enforcement failure Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented oversight scandal Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: A firearms trafficking investigation allowed guns to move into criminal networks, causing a major federal scandal. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Fast and Furious was designed to trace trafficking networks, but the operation became a warning about controlled-risk strategies that lose control. When guns connected to the operation were linked to violence, the scandal became a symbol of bureaucratic failure, political blame and weak oversight.
