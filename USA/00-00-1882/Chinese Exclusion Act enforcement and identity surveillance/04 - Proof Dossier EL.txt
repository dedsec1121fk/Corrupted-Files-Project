Chinese Exclusion Act enforcement and identity surveillance — ΦΑΚΕΛΟΣ ΑΠΟΔΕΙΞΕΩΝ

Τεκμηρίωση: DOCUMENTED: statute, case files, immigration records

Πηγές:
National Archives Chinese Exclusion Act case files; Library of Congress historical statutes.

Πρώτο γεγονός προς επαλήθευση:
The case shows how immigration control became a racialized surveillance system through exclusion, interrogations, certificates, detention, and family separation.

Θεσμικό ερώτημα:
It matters because identity paperwork became a tool for suspicion long before digital identity systems existed.

Ανοιχτό/αμφισβητούμενο σημείο:
Some enforcement records are incomplete, but the law and administrative burden are documented.
