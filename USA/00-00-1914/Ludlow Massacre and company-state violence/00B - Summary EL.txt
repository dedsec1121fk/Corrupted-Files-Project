Ludlow Massacre and company-state violence ========================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1914 Κατηγορία: labor violence / corporate power Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented historical atrocity Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Striking coal miners and their families in Colorado were attacked during a labor conflict involving company guards and the National Guard. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Ludlow case shows how a labor dispute became a battlefield. Workers were fighting for pay, safety, union recognition and dignity. Company power, private guards, state militia and public order rhetoric converged. The deaths of women and children in a tent colony made Ludlow one of the darkest symbols of American labor history.
