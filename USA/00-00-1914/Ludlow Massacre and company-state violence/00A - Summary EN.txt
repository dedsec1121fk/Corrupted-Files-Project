Ludlow Massacre and company-state violence ========================================== Country: USA Year folder: 1914 Category: labor violence / corporate power Evidence level: documented historical atrocity Core story ---------- Striking coal miners and their families in Colorado were attacked during a labor conflict involving company guards and the National Guard. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The Ludlow case shows how a labor dispute became a battlefield. Workers were fighting for pay, safety, union recognition and dignity. Company power, private guards, state militia and public order rhetoric converged. The deaths of women and children in a tent colony made Ludlow one of the darkest symbols of American labor history.
