George Floyd, police accountability, protests and institutional response (2020-2024) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 2020 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Policing, force and institutional accountability FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER One murder became a global demand for accountability because it revealed force, discipline, race and municipal risk in one image. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is coercive power backed by uniforms, emergency language, prison authority, border authority or public-order narratives. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
