George Floyd murder and protest response ======================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2020 Κατηγορία: policing / civil rights Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented murder and social crisis Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: A Minneapolis police officer murdered George Floyd, triggering nationwide and global protests. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case became a flashpoint because video showed prolonged force against a restrained man. It transformed policing debate, protest politics, municipal budgets, union power and racial justice narratives. It also triggered heavy law-enforcement responses and misinformation around protests.
