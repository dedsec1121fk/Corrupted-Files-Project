COVID nursing home deaths and policy accountability =================================================== Country: USA Year folder: 2020 Category: public health / governance Evidence level: documented controversy Core story ---------- Several US states faced scrutiny over nursing home COVID deaths, data reporting and hospital discharge policies. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The nursing-home crisis sits at the intersection of panic, capacity limits, vulnerable populations and political messaging. Facilities already had staffing and infection-control weaknesses. Policy choices about transfers and reporting became symbols of whether leaders protected the elderly or protected reputations.
