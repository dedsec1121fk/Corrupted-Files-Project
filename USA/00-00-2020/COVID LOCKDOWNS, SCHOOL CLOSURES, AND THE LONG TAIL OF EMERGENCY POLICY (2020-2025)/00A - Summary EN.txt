COVID LOCKDOWNS, SCHOOL CLOSURES, AND THE LONG TAIL OF EMERGENCY POLICY (2020-2025) Classification: [DOCUMENTED] Why this file exists: A serious pandemic was real. So were the heavy policy responses. The strongest evidence-based critique is not that every COVID measure was malicious. It is that some emergency tools, especially prolonged school disruption and broad social restrictions, produced long-tail damage that institutions only admitted more clearly later. What is documented: - UNESCO says the pandemic disrupted the education of more than 1.6 billion learners at its peak, calling it the most severe disruption to education systems in history. - WHO Europe warned that school closures had serious effects on children's education, development, mental well-being, and equality. - WHO/HBSC data later found a strong link between prolonged school closures and increased school pressure,...
