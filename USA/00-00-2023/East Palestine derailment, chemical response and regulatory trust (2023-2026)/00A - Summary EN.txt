East Palestine derailment, chemical response and regulatory trust (2023-2026) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 2023 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Public health, science and consent FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER A chemical train derailment became a trust crisis around evacuation, exposure, railroad power and long-term uncertainty. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is unequal knowledge: officials, corporations or experts hold risk information while affected people live with incomplete truth. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
