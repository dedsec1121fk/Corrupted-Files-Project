Core story
----------
A Norfolk Southern train derailment in East Palestine, Ohio released hazardous chemicals and
sparked distrust over safety and health claims.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The derailment became a national symbol because residents faced confusing messages,
controlled burns, corporate assurances and long-term uncertainty. The case shows how
chemical risk is not only measured in parts per million; it is also measured in whether
people believe institutions are telling the truth.

Timeline

- 2023: cleanup and investigations continue.
- Aftermath: rail safety debate grows.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------

- Controlled burn decision drew criticism.
- Settlements and regulatory scrutiny followed.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It connects rail deregulation, corporate safety culture and environmental trust.

Competing explanations and caution

documented.
