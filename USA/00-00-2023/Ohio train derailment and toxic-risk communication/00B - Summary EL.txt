Ohio train derailment and toxic-risk communication ================================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2023 Κατηγορία: environment / corporate safety Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented disaster Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: A Norfolk Southern train derailment in East Palestine, Ohio released hazardous chemicals and sparked distrust over safety and health claims. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The derailment became a national symbol because residents faced confusing messages, controlled burns, corporate assurances and long-term uncertainty. The case shows how chemical risk is not only measured in parts per million; it is also measured in whether people believe institutions are telling the truth.
