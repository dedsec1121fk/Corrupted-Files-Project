Ohio train derailment and toxic-risk communication ================================================== Country: USA Year folder: 2023 Category: environment / corporate safety Evidence level: documented disaster Core story ---------- A Norfolk Southern train derailment in East Palestine, Ohio released hazardous chemicals and sparked distrust over safety and health claims. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The derailment became a national symbol because residents faced confusing messages, controlled burns, corporate assurances and long-term uncertainty. The case shows how chemical risk is not only measured in parts per million; it is also measured in whether people believe institutions are telling the truth.
