Iran Contra affair
==================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1986
Κατηγορία: covert policy / executive power
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented scandal

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: US officials secretly facilitated arms sales to Iran and diverted proceeds
to Contra rebels despite legal restrictions.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Iran-Contra exposed a shadow foreign policy: classified networks, deniable
actors, money channels and ideological goals operating around congressional limits. It
showed how executive power can treat the law as an obstacle to route around, not a boundary.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1986: scandal becomes public.
- 1987: congressional hearings.
- 1990s: pardons close many legal consequences.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Arms sales to Iran contradicted public policy.
- Funds supported Contra rebels in Nicaragua.
- Hearings and prosecutions followed, with later pardons.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is a classic file about secrecy, deniability and constitutional limits.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Some participants framed actions as anti-communist necessity or hostage diplomacy.
The scandal remains documented as a serious breach of law and accountability.
