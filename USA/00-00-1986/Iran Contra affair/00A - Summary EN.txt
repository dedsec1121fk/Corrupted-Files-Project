Iran Contra affair ================== Country: USA Year folder: 1986 Category: covert policy / executive power Evidence level: documented scandal Core story ---------- US officials secretly facilitated arms sales to Iran and diverted proceeds to Contra rebels despite legal restrictions. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Iran-Contra exposed a shadow foreign policy: classified networks, deniable actors, money channels and ideological goals operating around congressional limits. It showed how executive power can treat the law as an obstacle to route around, not a boundary. Timeline -------- - 1986: scandal becomes public. - 1987: congressional hearings. - 1990s: pardons close many legal consequences.
