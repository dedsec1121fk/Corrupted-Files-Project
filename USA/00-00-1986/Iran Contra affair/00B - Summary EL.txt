Iran Contra affair ================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1986 Κατηγορία: covert policy / executive power Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented scandal Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: US officials secretly facilitated arms sales to Iran and diverted proceeds to Contra rebels despite legal restrictions. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Iran-Contra exposed a shadow foreign policy: classified networks, deniable actors, money channels and ideological goals operating around congressional limits. It showed how executive power can treat the law as an obstacle to route around, not a boundary. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 1986: scandal becomes public. - 1987: congressional hearings. - 1990s: pardons close many legal consequences.
