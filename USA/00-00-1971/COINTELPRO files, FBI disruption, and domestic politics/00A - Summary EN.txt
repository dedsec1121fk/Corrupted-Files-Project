COINTELPRO files, FBI disruption, and domestic politics Country: USA Year: 1971 Category: domestic surveillance / political disruption Evidence level: documented FBI programs exposed by files and congressional investigation Full story ---------- COINTELPRO moved beyond surveillance into disruption of civil-rights, antiwar, Black liberation, socialist, and other political groups. The public-trust issue is that state security power was used not only to observe possible crimes but to shape political life. Confirmed record ---------------- - The 1971 burglary of an FBI office helped expose files. - The Church Committee later documented FBI domestic abuses. - Targets included Martin Luther King Jr., Black activists, antiwar groups, and others.
