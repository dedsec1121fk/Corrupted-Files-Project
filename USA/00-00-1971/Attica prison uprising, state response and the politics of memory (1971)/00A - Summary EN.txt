Attica prison uprising, state response and the politics of memory (1971) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 1971 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Policing, force and institutional accountability FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER The uprising and deadly state assault show how institutions govern invisible populations and then fight over narrative and responsibility. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is coercive power backed by uniforms, emergency language, prison authority, border authority or public-order narratives. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
