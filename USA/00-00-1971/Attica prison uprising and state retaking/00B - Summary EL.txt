Attica prison uprising and state retaking ========================================= Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1971 Κατηγορία: prison system / state violence Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented deadly crackdown Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: New York authorities retook Attica prison after an uprising, leaving prisoners and hostages dead. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Attica began from prison conditions, racial tension and demands for dignity. The retaking became a bloodbath. Official narratives initially blamed prisoners for deaths that ballistics and later investigations contradicted. The file is a textbook example of how the first official story after violence can be wrong and how corrections may come too late.
