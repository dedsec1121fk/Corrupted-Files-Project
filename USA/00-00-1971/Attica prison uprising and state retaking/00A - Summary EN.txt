Attica prison uprising and state retaking ========================================= Country: USA Year folder: 1971 Category: prison system / state violence Evidence level: documented deadly crackdown Core story ---------- New York authorities retook Attica prison after an uprising, leaving prisoners and hostages dead. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Attica began from prison conditions, racial tension and demands for dignity. The retaking became a bloodbath. Official narratives initially blamed prisoners for deaths that ballistics and later investigations contradicted. The file is a textbook example of how the first official story after violence can be wrong and how corrections may come too late.
