Pentagon Papers and the secret history of Vietnam
=================================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1971
Κατηγορία: secrecy / press freedom
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented leak and court battle

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: A classified study showed US leaders had misled the public about Vietnam;
its publication triggered a landmark press-freedom fight.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Pentagon Papers revealed not a single lie but a pattern of managed
public narrative across administrations. The state argued publication endangered security.
The press argued citizens could not judge war without truth. The Supreme Court case became a
defining moment for prior restraint and the role of newspapers against government secrecy.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1971: papers published.
- 1971: Supreme Court rules in major press case.
- Aftermath: distrust of official war narratives deepens.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Daniel Ellsberg leaked the study.
- New York Times and Washington Post faced legal pressure.
- The Supreme Court allowed publication to continue.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It demonstrates how democratic consent collapses when war information is hidden or
manipulated.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Some details were genuinely sensitive, but the public-interest value was enormous.
