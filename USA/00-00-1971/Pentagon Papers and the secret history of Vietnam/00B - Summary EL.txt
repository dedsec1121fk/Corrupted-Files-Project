Pentagon Papers and the secret history of Vietnam ================================================= Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1971 Κατηγορία: secrecy / press freedom Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented leak and court battle Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: A classified study showed US leaders had misled the public about Vietnam; its publication triggered a landmark press-freedom fight. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Pentagon Papers revealed not a single lie but a pattern of managed public narrative across administrations. The state argued publication endangered security. The press argued citizens could not judge war without truth. The Supreme Court case became a defining moment for prior restraint and the role of newspapers against government secrecy.
