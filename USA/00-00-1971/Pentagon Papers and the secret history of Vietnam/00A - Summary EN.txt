Pentagon Papers and the secret history of Vietnam ================================================= Country: USA Year folder: 1971 Category: secrecy / press freedom Evidence level: documented leak and court battle Core story ---------- A classified study showed US leaders had misled the public about Vietnam; its publication triggered a landmark press-freedom fight. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The Pentagon Papers revealed not a single lie but a pattern of managed public narrative across administrations. The state argued publication endangered security. The press argued citizens could not judge war without truth. The Supreme Court case became a defining moment for prior restraint and the role of newspapers against government secrecy.
