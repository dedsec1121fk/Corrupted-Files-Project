Family separation at the US border ================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2018 Κατηγορία: immigration / state power Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented policy harm Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: US border policy separated children from parents, creating a humanitarian and administrative crisis. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The policy used family separation as deterrence. The scandal was not only moral but bureaucratic: records were insufficient, reunification was chaotic, and children carried trauma. The case shows how administrative design can become punishment. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 2018: policy becomes public scandal. - 2018 onward: lawsuits and reunification efforts. - Long-term: trauma and accountability debate.
