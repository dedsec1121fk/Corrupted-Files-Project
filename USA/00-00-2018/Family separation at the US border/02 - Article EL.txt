Family separation at the US border
==================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 2018
Κατηγορία: immigration / state power
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented policy harm

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: US border policy separated children from parents, creating a humanitarian
and administrative crisis.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The policy used family separation as deterrence. The scandal was not only
moral but bureaucratic: records were insufficient, reunification was chaotic, and children
carried trauma. The case shows how administrative design can become punishment.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2018: policy becomes public scandal.
- 2018 onward: lawsuits and reunification efforts.
- Long-term: trauma and accountability debate.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Thousands of children were separated.
- Recordkeeping failures complicated reunification.
- Courts and watchdogs scrutinized the policy.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It belongs in the database as a modern example of policy cruelty hidden behind
legal categories.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Supporters framed it as enforcement; critics saw intentional trauma and rights
violations.

Πηγές / διαδρομή επαλήθευσης
----------------------------
- US Government Accountability Office: https://www.gao.gov/
- US Department of Justice public releases and court records: https://www.justice.gov/news
