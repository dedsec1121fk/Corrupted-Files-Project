Cambridge Analytica, Facebook data, and election-manipulation panic — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Facebook user data was harvested through an app ecosystem and used by political consulting networks.
- The scandal produced regulatory penalties, hearings, and platform-policy changes.
- It became a symbol of data power in elections.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- The actual electoral effect of microtargeting is debated.
- Company marketing claims may have overstated technical power.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says voters were hypnotized by algorithms; the stronger proof is data misuse, opacity, and unaccountable targeting.
- A structural theory is platform capture: private data infrastructure became political infrastructure before democratic rules caught up.

Source trail
------------
- FTC Facebook settlement materials
- UK Information Commissioner and parliamentary materials
- U.S. congressional hearing record

Evidence discipline: prefer court records, official inquiries, archival files, regulator findings, named investigative reporting, and primary documents over anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.
