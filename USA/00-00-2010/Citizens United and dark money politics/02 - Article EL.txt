Citizens United and dark money politics
=======================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 2010
Κατηγορία: elections / money power
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented legal turning point

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The Supreme Court decision changed campaign finance by expanding
independent political spending rights.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Citizens United did not create money in politics from nothing, but it
changed the legal terrain. Super PACs and dark-money networks grew in influence, making it
harder for ordinary voters to trace who funds persuasion. The case is not a secret
conspiracy; it is a public legal transformation with deep democratic effects.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2010: Supreme Court decision.
- 2010s: Super PAC and dark-money ecosystem grows.
- Ongoing: reform debates continue.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Decision protected independent expenditures.
- Political spending vehicles expanded afterward.
- Disclosure gaps remain a central issue.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It belongs in the database because visible law can still create hidden power.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Supporters frame the ruling as speech protection; critics see donor power and
disclosure failure.

Πηγές / διαδρομή επαλήθευσης
----------------------------
- US Senate hearings and reports: https://www.senate.gov/
