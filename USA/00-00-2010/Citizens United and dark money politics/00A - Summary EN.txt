Citizens United and dark money politics ======================================= Country: USA Year folder: 2010 Category: elections / money power Evidence level: documented legal turning point Core story ---------- The Supreme Court decision changed campaign finance by expanding independent political spending rights. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Citizens United did not create money in politics from nothing, but it changed the legal terrain. Super PACs and dark-money networks grew in influence, making it harder for ordinary voters to trace who funds persuasion. The case is not a secret conspiracy; it is a public legal transformation with deep democratic effects.
