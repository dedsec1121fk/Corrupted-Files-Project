Citizens United and dark money politics ======================================= Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2010 Κατηγορία: elections / money power Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented legal turning point Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The Supreme Court decision changed campaign finance by expanding independent political spending rights. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Citizens United did not create money in politics from nothing, but it changed the legal terrain. Super PACs and dark-money networks grew in influence, making it harder for ordinary voters to trace who funds persuasion. The case is not a secret conspiracy; it is a public legal transformation with deep democratic effects.
