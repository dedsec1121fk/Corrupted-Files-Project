Summary:
The Advisory Committee on Human Radiation Experiments was created to examine the U.S. government's historical record on radiation experiments involving human subjects.
Its final report says that between 1944 and 1974 the federal government sponsored several thousand human radiation experiments and related activities.

Why it matters:
- It is another major documented case where the state, science, secrecy, and vulnerable people overlapped.
- It shows that “the government ran dangerous human experiments and only admitted them later” is not just an internet claim.

What the official review is known for:
- looking across decades of federal activity;
- reviewing ethics, consent, secrecy, and responsibility;
- documenting that large numbers of experiments or related activities existed;
- forcing a retrospective reckoning with how Cold War priorities distorted human-subject protections.

Important caution:
