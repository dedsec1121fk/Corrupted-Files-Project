Port Chicago disaster, Black sailors, and mutiny trials Country: USA Year: 1944 Category: military disaster / racism / labor safety Evidence level: documented naval disaster and contested military justice Full story ---------- The 1944 Port Chicago explosion killed hundreds at a California naval ammunition facility. Black sailors who later refused unsafe loading work were tried for mutiny, making the case a public-trust file about segregation, military labor, command responsibility, and punishment of those who resisted danger. Confirmed record ---------------- - The explosion killed 320 people. - Many Black sailors were assigned dangerous ammunition-loading work in a segregated Navy. - Fifty sailors were convicted of mutiny after refusing to resume loading under unsafe conditions.
