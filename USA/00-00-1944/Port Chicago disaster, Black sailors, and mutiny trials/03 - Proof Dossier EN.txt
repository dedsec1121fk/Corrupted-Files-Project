Port Chicago disaster, Black sailors, and mutiny trials — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The explosion killed 320 people.
- Many Black sailors were assigned dangerous ammunition-loading work in a segregated Navy.
- Fifty sailors were convicted of mutiny after refusing to resume loading under unsafe conditions.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Military law treated refusal as mutiny; later civil-rights readings treat it as safety protest.
- The full degree of command negligence is read through naval procedures and training records.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- Some theories say the Navy made Black sailors disposable by design; segregation and unsafe assignment are documented, while intent must be argued carefully.
- The structural theory is racialized risk: dangerous work and punishment fell hardest on those with least power.

Source trail
------------
- U.S. Navy Port Chicago records
- Court-martial materials
- National Park Service Port Chicago history

Evidence discipline: prefer court records, official inquiries, archival files, regulator findings, named investigative reporting, and primary documents over anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.
