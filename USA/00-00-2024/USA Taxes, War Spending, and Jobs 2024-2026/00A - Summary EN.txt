USA Taxes, War Spending, and Jobs: 2024-2026 Why this file exists: A lot of people do not just ask about scandals. They ask a simpler question: Where does the money go, what does the state prioritize, and what kind of work future is being built? This file is not a claim that “all tax money is stolen.” It is a record of spending priorities, budget pressure, military allocation, and labor-market stress. 1) Taxes on labour are real, even if the USA is not the highest-tax OECD country. The OECD says the tax wedge for the average single worker in the United States was 30.1% in 2024. That was below the OECD average of 34.9%, but it still means a large share of labour cost is absorbed by taxes and social contributions.
