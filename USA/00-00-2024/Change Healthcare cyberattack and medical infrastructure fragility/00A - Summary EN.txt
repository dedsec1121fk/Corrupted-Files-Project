Change Healthcare cyberattack and medical infrastructure fragility ================================================================== Country: USA Year folder: 2024 Category: cyber / health systems Evidence level: documented cyber disruption Core story ---------- A ransomware attack on Change Healthcare disrupted billing, pharmacies and medical payment systems across the US. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The case is not a paranormal story; it is modern infrastructure horror. The attack showed that health care is now dependent on billing networks, authentication, clearinghouses and third-party vendors that ordinary patients never see.
