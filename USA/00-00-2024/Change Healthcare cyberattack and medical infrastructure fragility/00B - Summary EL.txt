Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case is not a paranormal story; it is modern infrastructure horror. A single digital intermediary became a choke point for hospitals, clinics and patients. The attack showed that health care is now dependent on billing networks, authentication, clearinghouses and third-party vendors that ordinary patients never see.
