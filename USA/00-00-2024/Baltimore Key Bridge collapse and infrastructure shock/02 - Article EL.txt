Baltimore Key Bridge collapse and infrastructure shock
======================================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 2024
Κατηγορία: infrastructure / emergency response
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented disaster

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The Francis Scott Key Bridge collapsed after a container ship strike,
killing workers and disrupting a major port route.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The collapse was a sudden disaster, not a proven conspiracy. But it belongs
in this database because it exposed fragility: maritime logistics, bridge protection, worker
safety, emergency response and supply-chain dependency. Disasters often attract wild rumors;
a serious file keeps the human and engineering facts central.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- March 2024: collision and collapse.
- 2024: recovery and investigation.
- Aftermath: rebuilding and liability questions.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Bridge collapsed after the Dali vessel struck a support.
- Workers on the bridge were killed.
- Port and regional logistics were disrupted.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It teaches how to separate infrastructure failure analysis from online panic.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Speculative claims about intentional sabotage require evidence; the collision and
collapse are documented.
