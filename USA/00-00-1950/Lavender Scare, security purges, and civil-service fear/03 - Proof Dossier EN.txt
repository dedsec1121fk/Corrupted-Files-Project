Lavender Scare, security purges, and civil-service fear — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Cold War security programs targeted suspected gay and lesbian federal workers.
- Thousands of careers were harmed through investigations, firings, or coerced resignations.
- The episode is now studied as a civil-liberties and LGBTQ-rights failure.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Exact counts vary because many cases were hidden by personnel files and silence.
- Security claims were often asserted rather than proved.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane is partly structural: officials did coordinate purges, but the wider myth of inherent blackmail risk was prejudice dressed as security.
- A careful reading follows memos, personnel rules, and testimony rather than rumor.

Evidence discipline
-------------------
Use primary records, official investigations, court files, regulator documents, and named reporting before anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.

Source trail
------------
- U.S. Senate and civil-service historical records
- National Archives LGBTQ federal employment materials
- Cold War security-state scholarship
