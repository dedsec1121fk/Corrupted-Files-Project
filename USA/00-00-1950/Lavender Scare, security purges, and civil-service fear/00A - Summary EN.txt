Lavender Scare, security purges, and civil-service fear Country: USA Year: 1950 Category: civil liberties / sexuality / security state Evidence level: documented purge climate with later historical recognition Full story ---------- The Lavender Scare treated LGBTQ people in government as security risks. It turned private life into a loyalty file and showed how the national-security state could fuse prejudice, blackmail fear, employment power, and moral panic. Confirmed record ---------------- - Cold War security programs targeted suspected gay and lesbian federal workers. - Thousands of careers were harmed through investigations, firings, or coerced resignations. - The episode is now studied as a civil-liberties and LGBTQ-rights failure.
