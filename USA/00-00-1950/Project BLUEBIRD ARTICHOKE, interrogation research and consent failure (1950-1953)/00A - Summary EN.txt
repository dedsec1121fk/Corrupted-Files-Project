Project BLUEBIRD/ARTICHOKE, interrogation research and consent failure (1950-1953) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 1950 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Public health, science and consent FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Before MKULTRA became famous, intelligence research explored drugs, hypnosis and coercive interrogation under secrecy and weak oversight. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is unequal knowledge: officials, corporations or experts hold risk information while affected people live with incomplete truth. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
