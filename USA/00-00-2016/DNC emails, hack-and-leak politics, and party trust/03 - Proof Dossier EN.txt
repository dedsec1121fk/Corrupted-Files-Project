DNC emails, hack-and-leak politics, and party trust — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- DNC emails were stolen and published during the 2016 campaign.
- U.S. intelligence and later indictments attributed the operation to Russian actors.
- The leaks triggered party leadership fallout and long-term election-security debate.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- The electoral effect is debated and cannot be reduced to one cause.
- The authenticity of many emails is separate from the ethics of theft and timed publication.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane splits: some deny foreign hacking, others claim the entire election result was mechanically produced by leaks. Both overreach without evidence.
- A structural reading is information laundering: stolen true material can still be used as an influence weapon.

Source trail
------------
- U.S. intelligence assessment on 2016 election interference
- Special Counsel indictment materials
- DNC and campaign public record
