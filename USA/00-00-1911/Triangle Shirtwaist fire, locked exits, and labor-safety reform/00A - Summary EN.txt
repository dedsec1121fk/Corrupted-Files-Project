Triangle Shirtwaist fire, locked exits, and labor-safety reform Country: USA Year: 1911 Category: labor safety / industrial disaster / regulatory reform Evidence level: documented industrial disaster and reform catalyst Full story ---------- The Triangle Shirtwaist fire killed garment workers in New York and exposed locked exits, weak safety rules, immigrant labor exploitation, and the cost of treating workplace risk as a private business matter. Confirmed record ---------------- - 146 workers died in the 1911 fire. - The disaster became a major catalyst for workplace-safety reform. - The victims were largely immigrant women and girls. Disputed or unresolved points ----------------------------- - The owners' legal liability and moral responsibility were not treated the same by the public. - Reform memory can obscure the years of ignored worker warnings before the disaster.
