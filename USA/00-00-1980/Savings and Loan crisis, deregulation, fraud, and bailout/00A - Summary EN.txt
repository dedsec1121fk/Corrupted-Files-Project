Savings and Loan crisis, deregulation, fraud, and bailout Country: USA Year: 1980 Category: financial regulation / fraud / public bailout Evidence level: documented financial crisis with prosecutions and public cost Full story ---------- The Savings and Loan crisis showed how deregulation, risky lending, political pressure, deposit insurance, and fraud could create a public-cost disaster before the 2008 crisis. It is a bridge file for understanding moral hazard in U.S. finance. Confirmed record ---------------- - Hundreds of thrifts failed during the crisis. - The cleanup cost taxpayers large sums through federal resolution mechanisms. - There were criminal prosecutions and major regulatory reforms.
