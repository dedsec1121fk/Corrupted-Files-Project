ARTICLE The FBI microscopic hair comparison scandal is one of the strongest documented examples of institutional failure inside forensic science. It did not require an alien program, a supernatural claim, or one cinematic mastermind. It required something more ordinary and more dangerous: courtroom confidence outrunning science. For decades, microscopic hair comparison was used to link suspects to crimes. Analysts compared hair samples under microscopes and testified about similarities. In many trials, that testimony carried the emotional weight of “science,” even when the method could not support the certainty implied in court.
