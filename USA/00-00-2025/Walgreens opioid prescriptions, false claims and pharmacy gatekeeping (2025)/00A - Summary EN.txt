Walgreens opioid prescriptions, false claims and pharmacy gatekeeping (2025) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 2025 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Public health, science and consent FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER The case focuses on pharmacies as gatekeepers for controlled substances and on how warning signals can be ignored in retail systems. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is unequal knowledge: officials, corporations or experts hold risk information while affected people live with incomplete truth. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
