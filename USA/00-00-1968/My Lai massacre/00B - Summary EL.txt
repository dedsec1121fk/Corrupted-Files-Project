My Lai massacre =============== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1968 Κατηγορία: war crimes / military accountability Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented war crime Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: US soldiers killed Vietnamese civilians in My Lai; the cover-up and limited accountability became a defining Vietnam War scandal. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: My Lai was not only a battlefield atrocity. It was also a chain-of-command and institutional truth case. Civilians were killed, reports were suppressed or minimized, and the public learned how military systems can protect reputation before victims. The eventual exposure showed the importance of whistleblowers, journalists and congressional scrutiny.
