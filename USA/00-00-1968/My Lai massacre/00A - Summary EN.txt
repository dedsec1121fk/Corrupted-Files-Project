My Lai massacre =============== Country: USA Year folder: 1968 Category: war crimes / military accountability Evidence level: documented war crime Core story ---------- US soldiers killed Vietnamese civilians in My Lai; the cover-up and limited accountability became a defining Vietnam War scandal. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ My Lai was not only a battlefield atrocity. It was also a chain-of-command and institutional truth case. Civilians were killed, reports were suppressed or minimized, and the public learned how military systems can protect reputation before victims. The eventual exposure showed the importance of whistleblowers, journalists and congressional scrutiny.
