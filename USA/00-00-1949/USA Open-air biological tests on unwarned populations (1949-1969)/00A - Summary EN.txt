USA: Open-air biological tests on unwarned populations (1949-1969) 1) The Army sprayed simulants in cities and other locations without informed public consent Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: A disturbing but well-documented part of U.S. Cold War history is the open-air testing of biological warfare simulants. GAO testimony in 1994 said that between 1949 and 1969 the Army conducted several hundred biological-warfare tests in which unaware populations were sprayed with bacterial tracers or simulants thought at the time to be harmless. Some tests covered large urban areas including San Francisco and St. Louis.
