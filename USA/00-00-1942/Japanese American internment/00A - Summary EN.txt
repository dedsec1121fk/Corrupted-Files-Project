Japanese American internment ============================ Country: USA Year folder: 1942 Category: wartime rights / mass detention Evidence level: documented government injustice Core story ---------- The US government forcibly removed and incarcerated Japanese Americans during World War II. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Executive Order 9066 enabled mass removal from the West Coast. Families lost homes, businesses and freedom without individualized evidence. Later government review concluded the policy was driven by race prejudice, war hysteria and failure of political leadership rather than military necessity. Timeline -------- - 1942: Executive Order 9066 signed. - 1942-1945: incarceration camps operate. - 1988: Civil Liberties Act apologizes and provides redress.
