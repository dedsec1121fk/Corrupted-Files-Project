Japanese American internment
============================

Country: USA
Year folder: 1942
Category: wartime rights / mass detention
Evidence level: documented government injustice

Core story
----------
The US government forcibly removed and incarcerated Japanese Americans during World War II.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
Executive Order 9066 enabled mass removal from the West Coast. Families lost homes,
businesses and freedom without individualized evidence. Later government review concluded
the policy was driven by race prejudice, war hysteria and failure of political leadership
rather than military necessity.

Timeline
--------
- 1942: Executive Order 9066 signed.
- 1942-1945: incarceration camps operate.
- 1988: Civil Liberties Act apologizes and provides redress.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Over 100,000 people of Japanese ancestry were incarcerated.
- Many were US citizens.
- The US later issued apology and reparations.

Why this case matters
---------------------
This is one of the clearest US examples of lawful paperwork producing unlawful injustice.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
The wartime government claimed security necessity; later findings strongly rejected that
blanket justification.

Source trail / suggested verification
-------------------------------------
- US National Archives: https://www.archives.gov/
