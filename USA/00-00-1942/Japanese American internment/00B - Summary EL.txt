Japanese American internment ============================ Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1942 Κατηγορία: wartime rights / mass detention Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented government injustice Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The US government forcibly removed and incarcerated Japanese Americans during World War II. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Executive Order 9066 enabled mass removal from the West Coast. Families lost homes, businesses and freedom without individualized evidence. Later government review concluded the policy was driven by race prejudice, war hysteria and failure of political leadership rather than military necessity. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 1942: Executive Order 9066 signed. - 1942-1945: incarceration camps operate. - 1988: Civil Liberties Act apologizes and provides redress.
