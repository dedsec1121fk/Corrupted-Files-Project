Japanese American internment as administrative machinery — ΦΑΚΕΛΟΣ ΑΠΟΔΕΙΞΕΩΝ

Τεκμηρίωση: DOCUMENTED: executive order, camp records, later reparations record

Πηγές:
National Archives Japanese American incarceration records; Civil Liberties Act records.

Πρώτο γεγονός προς επαλήθευση:
Fear, racism, military claims, and bureaucracy led to mass incarceration of Japanese Americans without individualized evidence.

Θεσμικό ερώτημα:
It matters because emergency powers can convert suspicion into mass administrative punishment.

Ανοιχτό/αμφισβητούμενο σημείο:
The government later acknowledged injustice through apology and reparations.
