Buck v. Bell, forced sterilization and the respectable face of eugenics (1927-1970s) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 1927 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Public health, science and consent FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Courts, doctors and administrators turned bodily control into public policy and gave coercive eugenics a legal language. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is unequal knowledge: officials, corporations or experts hold risk information while affected people live with incomplete truth. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
