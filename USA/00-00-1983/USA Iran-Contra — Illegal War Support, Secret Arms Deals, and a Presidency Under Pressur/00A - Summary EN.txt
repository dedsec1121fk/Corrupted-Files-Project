USA: Iran-Contra — Illegal War Support, Secret Arms Deals, and a Presidency Under Pressure (1983-1993) 1) A scandal that joined two operations that were both supposed to be constrained Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: Iran-Contra remains one of the clearest modern U.S. examples of covert policy being run around Congress and then collapsing into public scandal. The core structure was this: - members of the Reagan administration supported the Contra rebels in Nicaragua even after Congress passed restrictions under the Boland framework; - officials also arranged secret or illicit arms sales to Iran; - when Reagan later acknowledged that the two operations were connected, the scandal escalated sharply.
