USA: Watergate, Surveillance Abuse, and Presidential Cover-Up (1972-1977) 1) Break-in, false denials, abuse of power, and collapse of a presidency Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: Watergate began with the June 17, 1972 break-in at Democratic National Committee headquarters in the Watergate complex, but it expanded into a much wider scandal involving illegal surveillance, hush money, obstruction, abuse of executive power, and a constitutional crisis. National Archives material describes the affair as a national trauma and a test of the rule of law. Why it matters: Watergate is a key case where “it’s bigger than the break-in” turned out to be true. The scandal showed how intelligence-style tactics, covert political operations, and executive cover-ups can fuse into one system.
