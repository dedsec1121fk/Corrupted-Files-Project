Exxon Valdez oil spill, corporate safety, and ecological accountability — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The tanker spilled oil in Prince William Sound in 1989.
- Cleanup, litigation, and ecological monitoring continued for years.
- The disaster helped reshape oil-spill prevention and liability law.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Long-term ecological recovery is measured unevenly across species and habitats.
- Corporate blame includes both individual navigation failure and system design.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The theory lane says fines are priced into corporate risk; the documented question is whether liability actually changes behavior.
- A structural reading is that remote communities carry the damage while corporate systems negotiate the cost.

Evidence discipline
-------------------
Use primary records, official investigations, court files, regulator documents, and named reporting before anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.

Source trail
------------
- National Transportation Safety Board records
- Oil Pollution Act history
- Ecological monitoring and litigation record
