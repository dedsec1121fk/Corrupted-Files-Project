Roe v Wade overturned and data privacy fears ============================================ Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2022 Κατηγορία: rights / digital surveillance Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented legal shift Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The Supreme Court overturned Roe v Wade, triggering abortion bans and fears about digital evidence in reproductive prosecutions. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Dobbs changed law and also changed the meaning of ordinary data. Search histories, period apps, location records and messages became part of a new fear landscape. The case shows how digital infrastructure built for convenience can become evidence infrastructure when legal regimes shift.
