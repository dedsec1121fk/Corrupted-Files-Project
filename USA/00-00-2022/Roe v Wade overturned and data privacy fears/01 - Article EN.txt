Core story
----------
The Supreme Court overturned Roe v Wade, triggering abortion bans and fears about digital
evidence in reproductive prosecutions.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
Dobbs changed law and also changed the meaning of ordinary data. Search histories, period
apps, location records and messages became part of a new fear landscape. The case shows how
digital infrastructure built for convenience can become evidence infrastructure when legal
regimes shift.
