Roe v Wade overturned and data privacy fears ============================================ Country: USA Year folder: 2022 Category: rights / digital surveillance Evidence level: documented legal shift Core story ---------- The Supreme Court overturned Roe v Wade, triggering abortion bans and fears about digital evidence in reproductive prosecutions. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Dobbs changed law and also changed the meaning of ordinary data. Search histories, period apps, location records and messages became part of a new fear landscape. The case shows how digital infrastructure built for convenience can become evidence infrastructure when legal regimes shift. Timeline -------- - 2022: Dobbs decision released. - 2022 onward: state bans and litigation. - Ongoing: digital privacy reform debate.
