USA: Church Committee — Intelligence abuses exposed by Congress (1975-1976) 1) The moment many hidden programs stopped being "paranoia" Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: The Church Committee was the U.S. Senate investigation that publicly documented a wide range of intelligence abuses by the CIA, FBI, NSA, and other agencies. The committee did not prove every rumor that circulated in the Cold War era, but it confirmed that major abuses were real: illegal domestic surveillance, covert interference with political groups, assassination plotting, mail opening, secret watchlists, and weak oversight.
