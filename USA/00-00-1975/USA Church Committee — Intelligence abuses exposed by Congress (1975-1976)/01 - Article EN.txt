USA: Church Committee — Intelligence abuses exposed by Congress (1975-1976)

1) The moment many hidden programs stopped being "paranoia"
Status: [DOCUMENTED]

Summary:
The Church Committee was the U.S. Senate investigation that publicly documented a wide range of intelligence abuses by the CIA, FBI, NSA, and other agencies. The committee did not prove every rumor that circulated in the Cold War era, but it confirmed that major abuses were real: illegal domestic surveillance, covert interference with political groups, assassination plotting, mail opening, secret watchlists, and weak oversight.

Why it matters:
- It is one of the clearest official confirmations that large intelligence systems can drift into abuse when secrecy outruns accountability.
- It connected several topics that had been dismissed or minimized: COINTELPRO, NSA bulk-style interception, CIA domestic intrusion, and assassination plotting.
- It is a key bridge file for understanding why later scandals (MKULTRA, NSA bulk collection, political spying) felt believable to many citizens.

Core documented findings:
- The Senate says the Church Committee identified a "wide range of intelligence abuses" by the CIA, FBI, IRS, and NSA.
- The FBI's COINTELPRO program was used to "disrupt" political organizations, not merely investigate crimes.
- The committee identified programs previously unknown to the public, including NSA programs such as SHAMROCK and MINARET.
- The final reports argued that oversight mechanisms had failed and needed structural reform.

Detailed notes:
- SHAMROCK involved NSA access to telegram traffic from major U.S. communications companies.
- MINARET involved watchlisting and interception targeting anti-war activists, civil-rights figures, and others without ordinary criminal-process safeguards.
- The committee also investigated CIA assassination plots involving foreign leaders, helping move the topic from rumor to official record.

What is proved / what is not:
- PROVED: major intelligence abuses happened, and Congress documented them.
- PROVED: COINTELPRO was real and aimed at disruption, not just passive observation.
- NOT PROVED by this file: every later modern surveillance claim is identical in scope or method.
- NOT PROVED by this file: one all-controlling hidden government. The record supports repeated abuse by institutions, not a single total theory of everything.
