Church Committee domestic spying revelations
============================================

Country: USA
Year folder: 1975
Category: intelligence oversight
Evidence level: documented congressional findings

Core story
----------
The Church Committee exposed CIA, FBI, NSA and IRS abuses including surveillance of
activists and covert action problems.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The hearings showed that intelligence agencies were not just foreign-facing machines. They
had watched, infiltrated, disrupted and catalogued Americans and political movements. The
importance of the committee is not only the abuses it found but the oversight architecture
it forced: intelligence committees, legal limits, and the idea that secret power still needs
democratic control.

Timeline
--------
- 1975-1976: Senate investigations run.
- Findings expose domestic spying and covert abuses.
- Aftermath: new oversight mechanisms emerge.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- COINTELPRO and other abuses entered mainstream record.
- Congress created stronger intelligence oversight structures.
- The hearings remain a reference point for surveillance debates.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It is a backbone file for every later surveillance scandal.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Some officials argued the programs were Cold War necessities. The documented abuses showed
necessity had become an elastic excuse.
