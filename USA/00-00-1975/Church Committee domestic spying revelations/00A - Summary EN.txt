Church Committee domestic spying revelations ============================================ Country: USA Year folder: 1975 Category: intelligence oversight Evidence level: documented congressional findings Core story ---------- The Church Committee exposed CIA, FBI, NSA and IRS abuses including surveillance of activists and covert action problems. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The hearings showed that intelligence agencies were not just foreign-facing machines. They had watched, infiltrated, disrupted and catalogued Americans and political movements. The importance of the committee is not only the abuses it found but the oversight architecture it forced: intelligence committees, legal limits, and the idea that secret power still needs democratic control.
