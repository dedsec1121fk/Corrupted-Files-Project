Church Committee domestic spying revelations ============================================ Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1975 Κατηγορία: intelligence oversight Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented congressional findings Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The Church Committee exposed CIA, FBI, NSA and IRS abuses including surveillance of activists and covert action problems. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The hearings showed that intelligence agencies were not just foreign-facing machines. They had watched, infiltrated, disrupted and catalogued Americans and political movements. The importance of the committee is not only the abuses it found but the oversight architecture it forced: intelligence committees, legal limits, and the idea that secret power still needs democratic control.
