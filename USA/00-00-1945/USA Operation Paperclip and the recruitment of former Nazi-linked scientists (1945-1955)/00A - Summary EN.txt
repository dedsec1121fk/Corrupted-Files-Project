USA: Operation Paperclip and the recruitment of former Nazi-linked scientists (1945-1955) 1) Real postwar recruitment, morally compromised by what it ignored or minimized Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: Operation Paperclip and related programs brought German and other foreign scientists, technicians, and engineers to the United States after World War II. National Archives material says more than 1,500 such personnel dossiers exist in declassified Defense records, while the Nazi War Crimes records project said 765 scientists, engineers, and technicians were brought to the U.S. between 1945 and 1955 under Overcast, Paperclip, and similar programs.
