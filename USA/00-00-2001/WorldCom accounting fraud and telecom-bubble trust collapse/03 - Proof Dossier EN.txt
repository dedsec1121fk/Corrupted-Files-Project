WorldCom accounting fraud and telecom-bubble trust collapse — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- WorldCom disclosed a massive accounting fraud and filed for bankruptcy.
- Executives were prosecuted and convicted.
- The scandal helped drive Sarbanes-Oxley-era corporate governance reform.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- The responsibility of auditors, analysts, and boards must be read separately from executive criminal acts.
- Telecom-bubble optimism made fraud easier to hide but did not cause every false entry.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says Wall Street knowingly sold fake growth; the record supports incentives and warning failures, but each actor needs documents.
- A structural reading is that capital markets reward narratives until accounting reality breaks them.
