PATRIOT Act and permanent emergency powers ========================================== Country: USA Year folder: 2001 Category: surveillance / counterterrorism Evidence level: documented law and policy shift Core story ---------- After 9/11 the US rapidly expanded surveillance, detention, financial monitoring and counterterror authorities. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The Patriot Act is a case about emergency law becoming normal infrastructure. Some powers addressed real threats; others expanded state reach into records, communications and association. The central concern is mission creep: tools born from extraordinary danger can remain after public fear fades.
