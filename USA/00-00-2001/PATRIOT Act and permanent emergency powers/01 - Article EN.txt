Country: USA

Core story
----------
After 9/11 the US rapidly expanded surveillance, detention, financial monitoring and
counterterror authorities.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The Patriot Act is a case about emergency law becoming normal infrastructure. Some powers
addressed real threats; others expanded state reach into records, communications and
association. The central concern is mission creep: tools born from extraordinary danger can
remain after public fear fades.

Timeline
--------
- 2001: law enacted.
- 2000s: use expands beyond initial public understanding.
- 2010s: Snowden disclosures revive debate.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Passed rapidly after 9/11.
- Expanded investigative and surveillance tools.
- Parts were later revised, challenged or allowed to expire.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It is necessary for understanding modern databases, watchlists and surveillance culture.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Supporters cite terrorism prevention; critics cite overbreadth, secrecy and chilling
effects.
