PATRIOT Act and permanent emergency powers ========================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2001 Κατηγορία: surveillance / counterterrorism Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented law and policy shift Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: After 9/11 the US rapidly expanded surveillance, detention, financial monitoring and counterterror authorities. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Patriot Act is a case about emergency law becoming normal infrastructure. Some powers addressed real threats; others expanded state reach into records, communications and association. The central concern is mission creep: tools born from extraordinary danger can remain after public fear fades.
