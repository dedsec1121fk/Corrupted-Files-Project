USA: Snowden, PRISM, and Section 702 Surveillance (2007-2026) 1) Content collection, incidental collection, and the normalization of mass-scale communications surveillance Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: Section 702 of FISA allows surveillance targeting of non-U.S. persons reasonably believed to be outside the United States, with compelled assistance from electronic communication providers. The modern public debate exploded after the Snowden leaks, which exposed PRISM and related collection systems to worldwide attention. PCLOB’s 2014 and 2023 reports, plus its 2026 follow-up and ODNI transparency reporting, confirm that the program is extensive, highly technical, and subject to recurring compliance and civil-liberties debates.
