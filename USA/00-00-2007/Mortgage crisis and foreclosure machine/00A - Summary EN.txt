Mortgage crisis and foreclosure machine ======================================= Country: USA Year folder: 2007 Category: financial corruption / systemic failure Evidence level: documented systemic failure Core story ---------- The financial crisis exposed predatory lending, securitization abuse, rating failures and foreclosure harms. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The crisis was not one villain but an ecosystem: mortgage brokers pushed risky loans, banks packaged them, rating agencies blessed structures, regulators lagged, and homeowners carried the damage. The scandal is that profits were privatized while collapse became public pain.
