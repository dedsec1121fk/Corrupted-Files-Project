Mortgage crisis and foreclosure machine ======================================= Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2007 Κατηγορία: financial corruption / systemic failure Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented systemic failure Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The financial crisis exposed predatory lending, securitization abuse, rating failures and foreclosure harms. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The crisis was not one villain but an ecosystem: mortgage brokers pushed risky loans, banks packaged them, rating agencies blessed structures, regulators lagged, and homeowners carried the damage. The scandal is that profits were privatized while collapse became public pain. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 2007: mortgage market stress deepens. - 2008: crisis becomes global. - Aftermath: Dodd-Frank and settlements. Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης...
