Rodney King beating and LAPD accountability =========================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1991 Κατηγορία: police violence / media evidence Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented police abuse Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: A videotaped beating by Los Angeles police officers exposed police violence to a national audience. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Rodney King case became a turning point because video pierced official narration. The public saw force that could not be easily reduced to paperwork. The acquittals in state court triggered the 1992 Los Angeles uprising, showing how one case can detonate years of accumulated grievance over policing, race and inequality.
