Gulf of Tonkin incident and war escalation
==========================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1964
Κατηγορία: war powers / intelligence
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented controversy

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Ambiguous and disputed naval incidents in the Gulf of Tonkin helped justify
massive escalation in Vietnam.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Tonkin story is about how uncertain intelligence can become political
certainty. One attack occurred; a second alleged attack became highly doubtful. Yet the
narrative supported congressional authorization and broad war powers. This file matters
because it shows how speed, secrecy and patriotic pressure can turn uncertainty into war.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- August 1964: reported attacks on US ships.
- Congress passes Tonkin Resolution.
- Later reviews challenge the official certainty.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- The Gulf of Tonkin Resolution expanded presidential war authority.
- Later disclosures cast serious doubt on the second alleged attack.
- The incident became a warning case for intelligence scrutiny.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is a key case for understanding intelligence claims before military escalation.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Debate focuses on knowledge, intent and timing. The larger lesson is that ambiguous
data should not be treated as blank-check authority.
