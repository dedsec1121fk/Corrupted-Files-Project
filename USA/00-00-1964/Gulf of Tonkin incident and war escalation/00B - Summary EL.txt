Gulf of Tonkin incident and war escalation ========================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1964 Κατηγορία: war powers / intelligence Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented controversy Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Ambiguous and disputed naval incidents in the Gulf of Tonkin helped justify massive escalation in Vietnam. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Tonkin story is about how uncertain intelligence can become political certainty. One attack occurred; a second alleged attack became highly doubtful. Yet the narrative supported congressional authorization and broad war powers. This file matters because it shows how speed, secrecy and patriotic pressure can turn uncertainty into war.
