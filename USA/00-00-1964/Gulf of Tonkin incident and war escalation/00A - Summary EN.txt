Gulf of Tonkin incident and war escalation ========================================== Country: USA Year folder: 1964 Category: war powers / intelligence Evidence level: documented controversy Core story ---------- Ambiguous and disputed naval incidents in the Gulf of Tonkin helped justify massive escalation in Vietnam. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The Tonkin story is about how uncertain intelligence can become political certainty. One attack occurred; a second alleged attack became highly doubtful. Yet the narrative supported congressional authorization and broad war powers. This file matters because it shows how speed, secrecy and patriotic pressure can turn uncertainty into war.
