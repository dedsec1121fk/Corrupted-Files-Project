Gulf of Tonkin, disputed attack, and war authorization Country: USA Year: 1964 Category: war powers / intelligence / deception claims Evidence level: documented incident with declassified doubts and disputed intent Full story ---------- The Gulf of Tonkin crisis became a classic case of intelligence, executive power, and war momentum. The first naval incident was real; the second alleged attack was later deeply questioned. Yet the political result was enormous: Congress passed a resolution that helped open the path to major U.S. escalation in Vietnam. Confirmed record ---------------- - The 1964 resolution passed after reported attacks on U.S. vessels. - Later declassified material and official histories cast serious doubt on the second attack. - The resolution became a broad legal-political basis for escalation.
