Bonus Army eviction =================== Country: USA Year folder: 1932 Category: veterans / state force Evidence level: documented state violence Core story ---------- World War I veterans seeking early bonus payments were driven from Washington encampments by federal troops. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The Bonus Army marched during the Depression because veterans needed relief. The government framed the camps as disorder and threat. The use of troops, cavalry, tanks and tear gas against former soldiers became a symbolic warning: even honored veterans could be treated as a security problem when they challenged fiscal policy. Timeline -------- - 1932: veterans gather in Washington. - Summer 1932: eviction ordered. - Aftermath: images damage trust in leadership.
