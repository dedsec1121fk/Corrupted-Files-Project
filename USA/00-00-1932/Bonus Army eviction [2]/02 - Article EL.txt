Bonus Army eviction
===================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1932
Κατηγορία: veterans / state force
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented state violence

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: World War I veterans seeking early bonus payments were driven from
Washington encampments by federal troops.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Bonus Army marched during the Depression because veterans needed relief.
The government framed the camps as disorder and threat. The use of troops, cavalry, tanks
and tear gas against former soldiers became a symbolic warning: even honored veterans could
be treated as a security problem when they challenged fiscal policy.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1932: veterans gather in Washington.
- Summer 1932: eviction ordered.
- Aftermath: images damage trust in leadership.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Veterans demanded early payment of promised bonuses.
- Federal troops cleared encampments.
- The event influenced public opinion during the Depression.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It shows how poverty, debt, honor and public order can collide in a crisis.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Authorities argued they were restoring order. Critics saw the action as excessive
and morally disastrous.
