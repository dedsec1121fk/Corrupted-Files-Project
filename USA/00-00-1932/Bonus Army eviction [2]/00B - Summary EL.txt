Bonus Army eviction =================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1932 Κατηγορία: veterans / state force Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented state violence Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: World War I veterans seeking early bonus payments were driven from Washington encampments by federal troops. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Bonus Army marched during the Depression because veterans needed relief. The government framed the camps as disorder and threat. The use of troops, cavalry, tanks and tear gas against former soldiers became a symbolic warning: even honored veterans could be treated as a security problem when they challenged fiscal policy.
