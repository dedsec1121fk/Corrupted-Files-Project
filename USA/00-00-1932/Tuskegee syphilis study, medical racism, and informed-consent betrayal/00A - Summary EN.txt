Tuskegee syphilis study, medical racism, and informed-consent betrayal Country: USA Year: 1932 Category: medical abuse / racism / public health Evidence level: documented unethical government medical study Full story ---------- The Tuskegee study followed Black men with syphilis without proper informed consent and continued after penicillin became standard treatment. It is one of the clearest U.S. cases where official medicine, racism, and research authority combined into long-term abuse. Confirmed record ---------------- - The U.S. Public Health Service ran the study from 1932 until public exposure in 1972. - Participants were misled and did not receive treatment in the way modern ethics would require. - The case led to major reforms in human-subject research ethics.
