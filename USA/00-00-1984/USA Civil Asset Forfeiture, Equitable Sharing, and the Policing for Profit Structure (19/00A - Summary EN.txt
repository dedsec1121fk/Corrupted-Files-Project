But it is part of this archive because it creates a documented structural incentive: agencies can seize assets tied to alleged criminal activity, route proceeds through forfeiture funds, and share proceeds with state and local partners. Critics call this "policing for profit." Even without adopting every criticism, the profit-linked structure itself is real and official.
