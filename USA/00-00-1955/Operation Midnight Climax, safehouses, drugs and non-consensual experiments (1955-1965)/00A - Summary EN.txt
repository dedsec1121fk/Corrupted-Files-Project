BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is unequal knowledge: officials, corporations or experts hold risk information while affected people live with incomplete truth. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
