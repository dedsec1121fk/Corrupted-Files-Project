Iraq WMD intelligence failure
=============================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 2003
Κατηγορία: war / intelligence failure
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented intelligence and policy failure

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The US-led invasion of Iraq was justified partly through claims about
weapons of mass destruction that were not found as presented.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This case is about how intelligence uncertainty, political pressure,
defectors, media repetition and post-9/11 fear produced a war narrative. The failure was not
a minor paperwork mistake; it led to invasion, occupation, mass casualties and regional
destabilization.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2002-2003: WMD claims dominate debate.
- 2003: invasion begins.
- Post-invasion: inspections fail to confirm central claims.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- No active WMD stockpiles were found as publicly argued.
- Prewar intelligence was heavily criticized.
- The war reshaped the Middle East and US trust.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is one of the most important modern examples of evidence being overstated before
war.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Debate continues over deliberate deception versus systemic failure; both readings
point to severe accountability problems.

Πηγές / διαδρομή επαλήθευσης
----------------------------
- US Senate hearings and reports: https://www.senate.gov/
- US Government Accountability Office: https://www.gao.gov/
