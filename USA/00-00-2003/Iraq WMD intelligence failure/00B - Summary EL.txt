Iraq WMD intelligence failure ============================= Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2003 Κατηγορία: war / intelligence failure Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented intelligence and policy failure Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The US-led invasion of Iraq was justified partly through claims about weapons of mass destruction that were not found as presented. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This case is about how intelligence uncertainty, political pressure, defectors, media repetition and post-9/11 fear produced a war narrative. The failure was not a minor paperwork mistake; it led to invasion, occupation, mass casualties and regional destabilization.
