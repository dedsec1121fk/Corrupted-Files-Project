MOVE bombing in Philadelphia
============================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1985
Κατηγορία: policing / urban violence
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented state violence

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Philadelphia police dropped an explosive on a rowhouse occupied by MOVE
members, causing deaths and destroying homes.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The MOVE bombing is one of the most shocking domestic policing cases in
modern US history. The state treated a standoff as a militarized problem and used force that
burned a neighborhood. The dead included children. The case forces readers to confront how
policing can become collective punishment when a group is dehumanized as an enemy.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1985: police confrontation with MOVE escalates.
- Bomb dropped; fire spreads.
- Aftermath: commissions, lawsuits and public memory battles.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Eleven people died, including children.
- A city block burned.
- Philadelphia later apologized.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It belongs beside Waco, Ruby Ridge and other militarized domestic operations, but
it happened in a city neighborhood.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Officials framed it as a dangerous armed confrontation. The scale and method of
response remain the central outrage.
