MOVE bombing in Philadelphia ============================ Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1985 Κατηγορία: policing / urban violence Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented state violence Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Philadelphia police dropped an explosive on a rowhouse occupied by MOVE members, causing deaths and destroying homes. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The MOVE bombing is one of the most shocking domestic policing cases in modern US history. The state treated a standoff as a militarized problem and used force that burned a neighborhood. The dead included children. The case forces readers to confront how policing can become collective punishment when a group is dehumanized as an enemy.
