MOVE bombing in Philadelphia ============================ Country: USA Year folder: 1985 Category: policing / urban violence Evidence level: documented state violence Core story ---------- Philadelphia police dropped an explosive on a rowhouse occupied by MOVE members, causing deaths and destroying homes. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The MOVE bombing is one of the most shocking domestic policing cases in modern US history. The state treated a standoff as a militarized problem and used force that burned a neighborhood. The dead included children. The case forces readers to confront how policing can become collective punishment when a group is dehumanized as an enemy.
