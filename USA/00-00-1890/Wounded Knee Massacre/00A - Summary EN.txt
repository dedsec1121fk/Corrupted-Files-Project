Wounded Knee Massacre ===================== Country: USA Year folder: 1890 Category: state violence / indigenous history Evidence level: documented historical atrocity Core story ---------- Federal troops killed Lakota people near Wounded Knee Creek after years of dispossession and coercive policy. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The event is often described as a battle in older official language, but historical work and Native testimony treat it as a massacre rooted in broken treaties, forced reservation life, fear of the Ghost Dance movement, and military pressure. The file matters because it shows how state narratives can rename violence and how the archive can preserve official language while communities preserve trauma.
