Wounded Knee massacre and public memory — ΦΑΚΕΛΟΣ ΑΠΟΔΕΙΞΕΩΝ

Τεκμηρίωση: DOCUMENTED: military records, survivor accounts, public inquiries

Πηγές:
National Archives military records; Lakota survivor histories; public historical commissions.

Πρώτο γεγονός προς επαλήθευση:
This case reconstructs the killing of Lakota men, women, and children near Wounded Knee Creek and the later struggle over official memory.

Θεσμικό ερώτημα:
It matters because official language can describe violence as order while survivors remember terror, loss, and desecration.

Ανοιχτό/αμφισβητούμενο σημείο:
Arguments over labels continue, but the deaths and military operation are not seriously disputed.
