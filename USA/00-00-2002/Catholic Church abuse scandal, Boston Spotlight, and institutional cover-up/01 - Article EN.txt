Country: USA
Year: 2002
Category: abuse / religious institution / cover-up
Evidence level: documented abuse scandal with court files and settlements

Full story
----------
The Boston clergy-abuse revelations showed that institutional reputation, hierarchy, secrecy, legal settlements, and victim disbelief can protect offenders for years. The case is a core public-trust file because moral authority became a shield against accountability.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Investigative reporting and court files exposed patterns of abuse and reassignment.
- The scandal led to resignations, settlements, and wider investigations across dioceses.
- Victim testimony became central to public understanding.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Individual liability, institutional knowledge, and statutes of limitation vary by case.
- Religious belief should be separated from administrative cover-up.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------

- A structural reading is reputation capture: institutions with moral authority can make victims carry the burden of proof.
