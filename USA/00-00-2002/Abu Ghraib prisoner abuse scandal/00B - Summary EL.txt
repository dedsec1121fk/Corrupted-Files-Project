Abu Ghraib prisoner abuse scandal ================================= Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2002 Κατηγορία: war crimes / detention Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented abuse Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Photos from Abu Ghraib prison revealed abuse and humiliation of detainees during the Iraq War. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Abu Ghraib was not only a few photos. It was a detention-policy failure: vague rules, pressure for intelligence, dehumanization, contractor and military complexity, and weak accountability. The public images became icons because they made hidden detention practices visible. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 2003-2004: abuse discovered and reported. - 2004: photos become public. - Aftermath: prosecutions and global backlash.
