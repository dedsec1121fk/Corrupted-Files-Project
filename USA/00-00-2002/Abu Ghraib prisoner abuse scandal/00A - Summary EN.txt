Abu Ghraib prisoner abuse scandal ================================= Country: USA Year folder: 2002 Category: war crimes / detention Evidence level: documented abuse Core story ---------- Photos from Abu Ghraib prison revealed abuse and humiliation of detainees during the Iraq War. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Abu Ghraib was not only a few photos. It was a detention-policy failure: vague rules, pressure for intelligence, dehumanization, contractor and military complexity, and weak accountability. The public images became icons because they made hidden detention practices visible. Timeline -------- - 2003-2004: abuse discovered and reported. - 2004: photos become public. - Aftermath: prosecutions and global backlash.
