Abu Ghraib prisoner abuse scandal
=================================

Country: USA
Year folder: 2002
Category: war crimes / detention
Evidence level: documented abuse

Core story
----------
Photos from Abu Ghraib prison revealed abuse and humiliation of detainees during the Iraq
War.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
Abu Ghraib was not only a few photos. It was a detention-policy failure: vague rules,
pressure for intelligence, dehumanization, contractor and military complexity, and weak
accountability. The public images became icons because they made hidden detention practices
visible.

Timeline
--------
- 2003-2004: abuse discovered and reported.
- 2004: photos become public.
- Aftermath: prosecutions and global backlash.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Photographs exposed detainee abuse.
- Military investigations and prosecutions followed.
- Debate continues over senior-level accountability.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It shows how torture and humiliation destroy moral authority and fuel long-term blowback.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Officials often framed the abuse as misconduct by low-level personnel; critics argue policy
and command environment made it possible.

Source trail / suggested verification
-------------------------------------

- US Senate hearings and reports: https://www.senate.gov/
