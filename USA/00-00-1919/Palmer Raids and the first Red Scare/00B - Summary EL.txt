Palmer Raids and the first Red Scare ==================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1919 Κατηγορία: civil liberties / surveillance Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented government repression Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: US authorities raided, detained and deported suspected radicals during panic over communism and anarchism. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Attorney General A. Mitchell Palmer and federal agents used public fear after bombings and labor unrest to justify mass raids. Many targets were immigrants or political radicals. The case is a blueprint for how emergency rhetoric expands police power: labels first, raids second, legal questions later. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 1919: bomb scares and labor conflict intensify. - 1919-1920: raids target radical networks. - Afterward: civil-liberties backlash grows....
