Palmer Raids and the first Red Scare ==================================== Country: USA Year folder: 1919 Category: civil liberties / surveillance Evidence level: documented government repression Core story ---------- US authorities raided, detained and deported suspected radicals during panic over communism and anarchism. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Attorney General A. Mitchell Palmer and federal agents used public fear after bombings and labor unrest to justify mass raids. Many targets were immigrants or political radicals. The case is a blueprint for how emergency rhetoric expands police power: labels first, raids second, legal questions later.
