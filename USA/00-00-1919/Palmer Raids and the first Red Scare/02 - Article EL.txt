Palmer Raids and the first Red Scare
====================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1919
Κατηγορία: civil liberties / surveillance
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented government repression

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: US authorities raided, detained and deported suspected radicals during
panic over communism and anarchism.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Attorney General A. Mitchell Palmer and federal agents used public fear
after bombings and labor unrest to justify mass raids. Many targets were immigrants or
political radicals. The case is a blueprint for how emergency rhetoric expands police power:
labels first, raids second, legal questions later.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1919: bomb scares and labor conflict intensify.
- 1919-1920: raids target radical networks.
- Afterward: civil-liberties backlash grows.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Thousands were arrested in raids.
- Deportations included political targets.
- The raids helped normalize federal domestic intelligence work.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: This case helps explain later cycles of fear: communists, terrorists, migrants,
gangs, hackers, extremists.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Some radicals did advocate violence, but the raids swept far wider than proven
criminal conduct and damaged due process.
