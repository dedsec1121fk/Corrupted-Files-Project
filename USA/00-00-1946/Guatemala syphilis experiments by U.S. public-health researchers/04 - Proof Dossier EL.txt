Guatemala syphilis experiments by U.S. public-health researchers — ΦΑΚΕΛΟΣ ΑΠΟΔΕΙΞΕΩΝ

Τεκμηρίωση: DOCUMENTED: presidential commission and archival records

Πηγές:
Presidential Commission Guatemala study report; HHS historical materials.

Πρώτο γεγονός προς επαλήθευση:
Non-consensual infection and exposure experiments were conducted in Guatemala involving vulnerable groups.

Θεσμικό ερώτημα:
It matters because medical abuse was not only domestic; U.S.-linked researchers exported unethical experimentation into populations with less protection.

Ανοιχτό/αμφισβητούμενο σημείο:
Official review later condemned the experiments; individual experience records remain incomplete.
