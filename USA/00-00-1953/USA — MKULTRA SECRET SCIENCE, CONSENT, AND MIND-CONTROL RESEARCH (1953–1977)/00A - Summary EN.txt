USA — MKULTRA: SECRET SCIENCE, CONSENT, AND MIND-CONTROL RESEARCH (1953–1977) EVIDENCE STATUS: [DOCUMENTED] CIA program and Senate/public record. ARTICLE MKULTRA is one of the rare cases where the phrase “mind control program” is not internet exaggeration. It was a real CIA program involving covert research into drugs, interrogation, behavior, and human subjects. The most famous public symbol is LSD, but the deeper scandal is consent. People were exposed to experiments or research conditions they did not meaningfully understand or approve. The program became a permanent wound in public trust because it proved that intelligence agencies had treated human beings as instruments. MKULTRA grew out of Cold War fear.
