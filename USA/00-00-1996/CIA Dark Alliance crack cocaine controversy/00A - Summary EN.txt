CIA Dark Alliance crack cocaine controversy =========================================== Country: USA Year folder: 1996 Category: media / intelligence controversy Evidence level: contested but historically significant Core story ---------- A newspaper series alleged links between Contra networks, crack cocaine trafficking and communities harmed by drug markets. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The Dark Alliance controversy is best read as two files: the strongest documented parts about Contra-era drug trafficking concerns and intelligence blind spots, and the more disputed claims about intentional CIA targeting of Black communities. Official reviews criticized or narrowed claims but also acknowledged troubling contacts and failures.
