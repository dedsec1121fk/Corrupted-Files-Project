CIA Dark Alliance και διαμάχη για crack κοκαΐνη
===========================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 1996
Κατηγορία: μέσα ενημέρωσης / διαμάχη υπηρεσιών πληροφοριών
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: contested but historically significant

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: A newspaper series alleged links between Contra networks, crack cocaine
trafficking and communities harmed by drug markets.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The Dark Alliance controversy is best read as two files: the strongest
documented parts about Contra-era drug trafficking concerns and intelligence blind spots,
and the more disputed claims about intentional CIA targeting of Black communities. Official
reviews criticized or narrowed claims but also acknowledged troubling contacts and failures.
The case matters because communities already harmed by policing and drugs saw a believable
pattern of impunity.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1996: series published.
- Late 1990s: official reviews and media backlash.
- Long-term: case becomes symbol of distrust.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Gary Webb’s series ignited national controversy.
- CIA Inspector General reviews examined Contra-drug issues.
- Public debate over media responsibility and intelligence accountability continues.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is a trust-collapse case where partial documentation and community trauma
combined into a larger narrative.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Claims of deliberate CIA crack distribution require caution; documented tolerance,
contact and investigative failure remain serious enough for study.
