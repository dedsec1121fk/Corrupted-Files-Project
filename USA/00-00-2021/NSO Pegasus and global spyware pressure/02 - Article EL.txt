NSO Pegasus and global spyware pressure
=======================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 2021
Κατηγορία: surveillance / private cyber power
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented global scandal

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Pegasus spyware showed how private surveillance tools could target
journalists, activists and officials worldwide.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Although not solely a US case, Pegasus matters to the US database because it
changed American sanctions, diplomacy and technology policy. The scandal shows that
surveillance power no longer belongs only to states; private vendors can sell state-grade
intrusion to governments.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2021: Pegasus Project reporting.
- 2021 onward: sanctions and lawsuits.
- Ongoing: spyware regulation debate.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Pegasus can compromise phones.
- Journalists and activists were reported among targets.
- US placed NSO Group on an export-control entity list.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It connects cyber weapons, human rights, journalism and democratic oversight.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Attribution and individual targeting claims require careful forensic verification,
but the existence and abuse risk of mercenary spyware is documented.
