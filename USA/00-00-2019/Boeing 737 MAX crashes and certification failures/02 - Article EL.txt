Boeing 737 MAX crashes and certification failures
=================================================

Χώρα: ΗΠΑ
Φάκελος έτους: 2019
Κατηγορία: corporate regulation / aviation safety
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented corporate and regulatory failure

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Two deadly crashes exposed problems in Boeing’s 737 MAX design, training
assumptions and FAA certification oversight.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The 737 MAX scandal is a case about engineering culture under market
pressure. A system meant to prevent stalls became tied to fatal accidents, and regulators
had delegated significant oversight to the manufacturer. The human cost made visible a
deeper issue: safety systems can be weakened by schedule, competition and trust-based
certification.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2018: Lion Air crash.
- 2019: Ethiopian Airlines crash and global grounding.
- Aftermath: redesign, settlements and oversight changes.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Crashes in 2018 and 2019 killed 346 people.
- MAX fleet was grounded globally.
- Boeing faced criminal and civil consequences.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It shows how captured or delegated regulation can fail in high-stakes technology.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Responsibility is shared across design, disclosure, training, certification and
airline operation; simplistic single-cause claims are weak.
