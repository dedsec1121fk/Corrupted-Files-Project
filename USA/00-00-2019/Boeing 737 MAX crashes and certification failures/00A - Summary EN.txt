Boeing 737 MAX crashes and certification failures ================================================= Country: USA Year folder: 2019 Category: corporate regulation / aviation safety Evidence level: documented corporate and regulatory failure Core story ---------- Two deadly crashes exposed problems in Boeing’s 737 MAX design, training assumptions and FAA certification oversight. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The 737 MAX scandal is a case about engineering culture under market pressure. A system meant to prevent stalls became tied to fatal accidents, and regulators had delegated significant oversight to the manufacturer. The human cost made visible a deeper issue: safety systems can be weakened by schedule, competition and trust-based certification.
