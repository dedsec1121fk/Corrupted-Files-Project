Tulsa Race Massacre, destroyed wealth, and the long cover-up Country: USA Year: 1921 Category: racial violence / local cover-up Evidence level: documented massacre with long-suppressed accountability Full story ---------- In 1921, white mobs attacked Tulsa's Greenwood district, killing Black residents and destroying a prosperous community known as Black Wall Street. The public-trust issue is not only the violence. It is the official and civic silence that followed: insurance denials, missing accountability, buried memory, and generations of wealth loss.
