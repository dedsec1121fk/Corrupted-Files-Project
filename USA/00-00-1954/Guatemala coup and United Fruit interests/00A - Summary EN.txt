Guatemala coup and United Fruit interests ========================================= Country: USA Year folder: 1954 Category: foreign intervention / corporate influence Evidence level: documented covert intervention Core story ---------- The CIA backed the overthrow of Guatemala’s elected government after land reform threatened powerful interests and Cold War fears were invoked. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The coup against Jacobo Árbenz became a classic Cold War case: anti-communist language, covert operations, propaganda, exile forces and corporate interests overlapped. United Fruit’s land holdings and lobbying environment shaped Washington perceptions. The overthrow contributed to decades of instability and violence in Guatemala.
