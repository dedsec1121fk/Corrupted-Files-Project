Guatemala coup and United Fruit interests ========================================= Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 1954 Κατηγορία: foreign intervention / corporate influence Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented covert intervention Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The CIA backed the overthrow of Guatemala’s elected government after land reform threatened powerful interests and Cold War fears were invoked. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The coup against Jacobo Árbenz became a classic Cold War case: anti- communist language, covert operations, propaganda, exile forces and corporate interests overlapped. United Fruit’s land holdings and lobbying environment shaped Washington perceptions. The overthrow contributed to decades of instability and violence in Guatemala.
