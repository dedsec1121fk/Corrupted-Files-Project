USA: Operation CHAOS and HTLINGUAL (1952-1974) 1) Domestic spying, mail opening, and antiwar-era intelligence overreach Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: Two of the clearest examples of Cold War domestic intelligence overreach were HTLINGUAL and MHCHAOS (usually called Operation CHAOS). - HTLINGUAL was a CIA mail-intercept and mail-opening program run from 1952 to 1973, initially against Soviet-bloc mail and later touching large numbers of letters involving U.S. persons. - MHCHAOS began in 1967 after President Lyndon Johnson asked intelligence agencies to look for foreign backing behind the antiwar movement. It expanded into a broad CIA effort that collected information on American citizens and domestic groups despite the CIA's foreign-intelligence mandate.
