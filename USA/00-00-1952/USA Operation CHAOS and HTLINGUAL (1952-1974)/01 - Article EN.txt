USA: Operation CHAOS and HTLINGUAL (1952-1974)

1) Domestic spying, mail opening, and antiwar-era intelligence overreach
Status: [DOCUMENTED]

Summary:
Two of the clearest examples of Cold War domestic intelligence overreach were HTLINGUAL and MHCHAOS (usually called Operation CHAOS).
- HTLINGUAL was a CIA mail-intercept and mail-opening program run from 1952 to 1973, initially against Soviet-bloc mail and later touching large numbers of letters involving U.S. persons.
- MHCHAOS began in 1967 after President Lyndon Johnson asked intelligence agencies to look for foreign backing behind the antiwar movement. It expanded into a broad CIA effort that collected information on American citizens and domestic groups despite the CIA's foreign-intelligence mandate.

Why it matters:
These cases matter because they show that governments do not need futuristic technology to build a surveillance state. Old-fashioned state power already included secret mail opening, watch lists, and huge indexes on citizens engaged in lawful political dissent.

What is proved:
- CIA's own historical review says HTLINGUAL covered more than 2.7 million letters and opened more than 215,000 over 21 years.
- CIA's 2024 Studies in Intelligence article says internal reviews in the 1960s found HTLINGUAL did not justify the operational burden and political risk, and that James Schlesinger terminated it in 1973.
- The same CIA history says MHCHAOS began in August 1967 under presidential direction, and eventually produced a computer index of roughly 300,000 names and about 7,200 files on U.S. citizens and organizations.
- CIA press-archive items and later litigation records show HTLINGUAL became one of the major scandal areas examined during the 1970s intelligence investigations.

What this archive does NOT claim:
- It does NOT claim that every antiwar or civil-rights activist was personally targeted by covert collection.
- It does NOT claim that foreign influence in protest movements never existed. The documented point is that the intelligence dragnet went far beyond what was justified.

Detailed timeline:
- 1952: CIA begins a New York mail-intercept effort aimed at Soviet targets; the broader project later becomes known as HTLINGUAL.
- 1955-1956: James Angleton's CI Staff takes over and expands the operation; CIA begins systematic mail opening in New York.
- 1958: FBI becomes aware of HTLINGUAL and starts receiving reporting from it.
- 1961: CIA opens a dedicated New York facility for mail opening work.
- August 1967: MHCHAOS begins after Johnson orders agencies to examine possible foreign support for antiwar protests.
- 1967-1973: CIA, FBI, and NSA reporting is funneled into broad files and indexes on U.S. persons; CIA later concludes it found no major foreign control behind the protests.
- 1973: Schlesinger shuts down HTLINGUAL; Lew Allen shuts down MINARET (see separate file), and the climate shifts toward exposure.
- 1974-1976: press reporting and congressional investigations bring both operations into public view.
