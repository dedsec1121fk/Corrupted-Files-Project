USA: Operation PBSUCCESS — CIA-Backed Coup in Guatemala (1952-1954) 1) A declassified covert operation, not a theory Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: Operation PBSUCCESS is one of the best-known declassified examples of the CIA helping engineer regime change in another country. The CIA's own reading-room material describes PBSUCCESS as the covert action that supported the 1954 coup in Guatemala. The CIA FOIA Guatemala collection says it contains 5,120 documents and more than 14,000 pages covering CIA involvement in the 1954 coup. Why it matters: - It is a classic example of hidden U.S. intervention behind public anti-communist justification. - It shows that “the government overthrows foreign governments in secret” is not fantasy. - It demonstrates how intelligence agencies can shape another country's future while denying or minimizing their role publicly.
