Operation Northwoods false-flag proposal — ΦΑΚΕΛΟΣ ΑΠΟΔΕΙΞΕΩΝ

Τεκμηρίωση: DOCUMENTED: declassified Joint Chiefs proposal

Πηγές:
National Security Archive Northwoods PDF: https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/news/20010430/northwoods.pdf

Πρώτο γεγονός προς επαλήθευση:
Senior military planners proposed deceptive actions to justify intervention in Cuba; the proposal was not executed.

Θεσμικό ερώτημα:
It matters because it proves that false-flag style proposals existed in official planning, while not proving every later false-flag claim.

Ανοιχτό/αμφισβητούμενο σημείο:
The case is often misused; the accurate lesson is narrower but serious.
