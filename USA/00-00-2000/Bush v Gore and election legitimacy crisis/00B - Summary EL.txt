Bush v Gore and election legitimacy crisis ========================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2000 Κατηγορία: elections / judiciary Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented constitutional crisis Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The 2000 US presidential election was effectively decided after recount litigation reached the Supreme Court. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case shows how ballots, machines, deadlines, courts and legitimacy all meet under pressure. Florida’s recount chaos became a national trauma. The Supreme Court decision ended the recount, awarding the presidency in practical terms. The corruption question is not simply whether votes were stolen; it is whether systems were too fragile, partisan and legally inconsistent to produce trust.
