Bush v Gore and election legitimacy crisis ========================================== Country: USA Year folder: 2000 Category: elections / judiciary Evidence level: documented constitutional crisis Core story ---------- The 2000 US presidential election was effectively decided after recount litigation reached the Supreme Court. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The case shows how ballots, machines, deadlines, courts and legitimacy all meet under pressure. Florida’s recount chaos became a national trauma. The Supreme Court decision ended the recount, awarding the presidency in practical terms. The corruption question is not simply whether votes were stolen; it is whether systems were too fragile, partisan and legally inconsistent to produce trust.
