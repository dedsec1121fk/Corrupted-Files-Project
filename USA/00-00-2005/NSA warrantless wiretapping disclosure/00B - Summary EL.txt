NSA warrantless wiretapping disclosure ====================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2005 Κατηγορία: surveillance / executive power Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented secret program Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Journalists revealed warrantless surveillance authorized after 9/11 outside the ordinary court process. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case showed how counterterror logic created secret legal interpretations. Citizens were told surveillance had checks, while classified programs operated under narrower internal permission. It became a bridge between Patriot Act-era powers and later Snowden-era mass surveillance debates.
