ARTICLE Digital identity is not one single villain and not one single solution. It is an infrastructure battle. The same system that can reduce fraud, simplify paperwork, and let people prove age or identity without carrying many documents can also become a gatekeeping layer for travel, banking, work, speech, health services, or access to public life. This file treats REAL ID, mobile driver’s licenses, digital wallets, and age verification as a mixed case: documented policy, legitimate administrative goals, and serious civil-liberty risks.
