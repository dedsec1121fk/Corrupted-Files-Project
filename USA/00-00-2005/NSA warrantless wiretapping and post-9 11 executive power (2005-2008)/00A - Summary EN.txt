NSA warrantless wiretapping and post-9/11 executive power (2005-2008) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 2005 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Surveillance, secrecy and information control FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER The controversy showed how emergency legal reasoning can bypass ordinary surveillance procedures before the public understands what changed. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is secrecy plus asymmetry: one side collects, classifies, stores or shapes information while ordinary people cannot see the full system. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
