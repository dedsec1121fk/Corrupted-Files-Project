Ruby Ridge, federal force, rules of engagement and public trust (1992) COUNTRY: USA YEAR FOLDER: 1992 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Policing, force and institutional accountability FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER A federal standoff, deaths and disputed rules of engagement became a long-lasting symbol of unaccountable state force. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is coercive power backed by uniforms, emergency language, prison authority, border authority or public-order narratives. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
