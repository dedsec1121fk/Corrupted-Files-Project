Ferguson police shooting and municipal fines system =================================================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2014 Κατηγορία: policing / civil rights Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented DOJ findings Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The killing of Michael Brown triggered protests and a DOJ investigation into Ferguson’s police and municipal court practices. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Ferguson became more than one shooting. DOJ findings described a local system using fines, fees and enforcement in ways that burdened Black residents and funded government priorities. This turned a police case into a governance case: public safety was entangled with revenue extraction.
