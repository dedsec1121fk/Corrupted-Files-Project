Ferguson police shooting and municipal fines system =================================================== Country: USA Year folder: 2014 Category: policing / civil rights Evidence level: documented DOJ findings Core story ---------- The killing of Michael Brown triggered protests and a DOJ investigation into Ferguson’s police and municipal court practices. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Ferguson became more than one shooting. DOJ findings described a local system using fines, fees and enforcement in ways that burdened Black residents and funded government priorities. This turned a police case into a governance case: public safety was entangled with revenue extraction.
