Flint water crisis ================== Country: USA Year folder: 2014 Category: public health / environmental injustice Evidence level: documented government failure Core story ---------- A water-source switch and treatment failures exposed Flint residents to lead and unsafe water. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Flint is a public-health betrayal case. Residents complained about water quality while officials minimized risk. The scandal exposed emergency management, austerity logic, environmental racism and the gap between technical warnings and political response. Timeline -------- - 2014: water source switched. - 2015: crisis becomes national. - Aftermath: lawsuits, settlements and long recovery.
