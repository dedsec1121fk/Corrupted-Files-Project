Flint water crisis ================== Χώρα: ΗΠΑ Φάκελος έτους: 2014 Κατηγορία: public health / environmental injustice Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented government failure Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: A water-source switch and treatment failures exposed Flint residents to lead and unsafe water. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Flint is a public-health betrayal case. Residents complained about water quality while officials minimized risk. The scandal exposed emergency management, austerity logic, environmental racism and the gap between technical warnings and political response. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 2014: water source switched. - 2015: crisis becomes national. - Aftermath: lawsuits, settlements and long recovery.
