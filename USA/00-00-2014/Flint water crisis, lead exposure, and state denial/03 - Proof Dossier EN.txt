Flint water crisis, lead exposure, and state denial — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Lead contamination and water-quality failures were documented by researchers, residents, and official reviews.
- The crisis involved emergency management decisions, state oversight, and public-health communication failures.
- Civil settlements and criminal/legal proceedings followed.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Legal responsibility for individual officials has been contested in court.
- The long-term health impact is measured through population studies, not a single simple number.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- Some theories say the crisis was a deliberate poisoning experiment; the documented record supports reckless cost-cutting and denial more strongly than an experiment claim.
- A structural conspiracy reading is stronger: poor, majority-Black residents were treated as politically disposable.

Source trail
------------
- Michigan Civil Rights Commission report
- EPA and state water-quality records
- Flint Water Advisory Task Force report

Evidence discipline: court findings, official reports, regulatory records, parliamentary files, and named investigative reporting are stronger than rumor, anonymous posts, or claims that require a hidden master plan.
