Lambrakis assassination and the parastate ========================================= Country: Greece Year folder: 1963 Category: political violence / parastate Evidence level: documented political scandal Core story ---------- The killing of MP Grigoris Lambrakis exposed links between far-right violence, police tolerance and a deeper parastate. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Lambrakis was not just a murder victim; he became evidence of a shadow system. The investigation suggested that official democracy had unofficial enforcers. The case helped prepare public understanding of the later dictatorship because it showed that anti- democratic networks already existed beneath formal institutions.
