Lambrakis assassination and the parastate ========================================= Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1963 Κατηγορία: political violence / parastate Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented political scandal Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The killing of MP Grigoris Lambrakis exposed links between far-right violence, police tolerance and a deeper parastate. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Lambrakis was not just a murder victim; he became evidence of a shadow system. The investigation suggested that official democracy had unofficial enforcers. The case helped prepare public understanding of the later dictatorship because it showed that anti-democratic networks already existed beneath formal institutions.
