GREECE — COVID SMS PERMITS: LOCKDOWNS, CERTIFICATES, AND PERMISSION CULTURE (2020–2023) EVIDENCE STATUS: [DOCUMENTED POLICY PERIOD] with retrospective civil-liberty analysis. ARTICLE The Greek COVID control period was a real emergency and a real civil-liberty shock. Both truths must be kept together. The virus killed people and pressured hospitals. At the same time, the state introduced movement permissions, SMS codes, business closures, school restrictions, mask rules, vaccination certificates, fines, police checks, and daily public messaging that changed the relationship between citizen and state. The most symbolic Greek detail was the SMS movement system. Citizens sent a code to receive permission for specific categories of movement, such as work, pharmacy, groceries, exercise, assistance, or essential reasons.
