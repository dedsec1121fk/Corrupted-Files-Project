Athens Polytechnic uprising, tank assault, and state-violence memory — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The uprising and military-police repression are documented historical events.
- The tank entry into the Polytechnic became an iconic image of dictatorship violence.
- The event accelerated the regime crisis, even though the dictatorship did not fall immediately that night.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Casualty lists, responsibility chains, and later commemorative narratives require careful source work.
- Political groups have repeatedly fought over ownership of the memory.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- Denialist theories try to erase deaths or minimize repression; they conflict with the documented record of violence and regime accountability.
- A structural reading is that authoritarian states attack truth after attacking bodies.

Evidence discipline
-------------------
Use primary records, official investigations, court files, regulator documents, and named reporting before anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.

Source trail
------------
- Greek historical archives on the Polytechnic uprising
- Court and transition-era accountability material
- Student radio and testimony records
