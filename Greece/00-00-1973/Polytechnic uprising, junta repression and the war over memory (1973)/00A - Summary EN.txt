Polytechnic uprising, junta repression and the war over memory (1973) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 1973 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Policing, force and institutional accountability FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER The Polytechnic is a state-violence file and a memory file about resistance, repression and later fights over symbolism and denial. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is coercive power backed by uniforms, emergency language, prison authority, border authority or public-order narratives. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
