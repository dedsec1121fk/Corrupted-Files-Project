Zahopoulos affair, media blackmail, and ministerial power

Country: Greece
Year: 2007
Category: media scandal / political access / alleged blackmail
Evidence level: documented public scandal with disputed criminal and political layers

Full story
----------
The Zahopoulos affair mixed a senior culture-ministry official, alleged blackmail material, media intermediaries, suicide attempt, political embarrassment, and questions about how cultural funds, appointments, and access operated behind official language.

Confirmed record
----------------
- The scandal entered public view after a suicide attempt and intense media coverage.
- Investigations examined alleged blackmail and political/media handling.
- The case raised questions about power networks around the Culture Ministry.

Disputed or unresolved points
