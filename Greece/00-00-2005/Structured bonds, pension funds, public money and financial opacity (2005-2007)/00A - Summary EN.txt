Structured bonds, pension funds, public money and financial opacity (2005-2007) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 2005 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Corruption, capture and public money FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Pension money was exposed to complex products, intermediaries and political responsibility, making opacity itself the weapon. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is networks of contracts, favors, media protection, political access, weak audits and delayed courts. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
