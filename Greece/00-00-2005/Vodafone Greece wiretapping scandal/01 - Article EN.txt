Vodafone Greece wiretapping scandal
===================================

Country: Greece
Year folder: 2005
Category: surveillance / telecoms
Evidence level: documented wiretapping scandal

Core story
----------
A covert interception system targeted the phones of senior Greek officials, including the
prime minister.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
This is one of Greece’s most important surveillance files. Unknown operators exploited
lawful-intercept capabilities inside telecom infrastructure. The scandal shows the danger of
building interception features: even if meant for lawful use, they can become hidden attack
surfaces. The death of Vodafone engineer Kostas Tsalikidis added lasting suspicion and
grief.

Timeline
--------
- 2004-2005: interceptions discovered.
- 2005: engineer death deepens mystery.
- Afterward: investigations and unresolved attribution.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Phones of senior officials were targeted.
- Lawful-intercept technology was abused.
- The case remains symbolically unresolved.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It is essential for understanding later Greek spyware and EYP controversies.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Attribution remains disputed; the existence of the interception is documented.
