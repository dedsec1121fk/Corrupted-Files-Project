Vodafone Greece wiretapping scandal =================================== Country: Greece Year folder: 2005 Category: surveillance / telecoms Evidence level: documented wiretapping scandal Core story ---------- A covert interception system targeted the phones of senior Greek officials, including the prime minister. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ This is one of Greece’s most important surveillance files. Unknown operators exploited lawful-intercept capabilities inside telecom infrastructure. The scandal shows the danger of building interception features: even if meant for lawful use, they can become hidden attack surfaces. The death of Vodafone engineer Kostas Tsalikidis added lasting suspicion and grief.
