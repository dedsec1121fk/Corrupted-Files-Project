Vodafone Greece wiretapping scandal =================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 2005 Κατηγορία: surveillance / telecoms Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented wiretapping scandal Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: A covert interception system targeted the phones of senior Greek officials, including the prime minister. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This is one of Greece’s most important surveillance files. Unknown operators exploited lawful-intercept capabilities inside telecom infrastructure. The scandal shows the danger of building interception features: even if meant for lawful use, they can become hidden attack surfaces.
