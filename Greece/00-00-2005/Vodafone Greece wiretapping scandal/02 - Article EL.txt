Vodafone Greece wiretapping scandal
===================================

Χώρα: Ελλάδα
Φάκελος έτους: 2005
Κατηγορία: surveillance / telecoms
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented wiretapping scandal

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: A covert interception system targeted the phones of senior Greek officials,
including the prime minister.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This is one of Greece’s most important surveillance files. Unknown operators
exploited lawful-intercept capabilities inside telecom infrastructure. The scandal shows the
danger of building interception features: even if meant for lawful use, they can become
hidden attack surfaces. The death of Vodafone engineer Kostas Tsalikidis added lasting
suspicion and grief.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2004-2005: interceptions discovered.
- 2005: engineer death deepens mystery.
- Afterward: investigations and unresolved attribution.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Phones of senior officials were targeted.
- Lawful-intercept technology was abused.
- The case remains symbolically unresolved.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is essential for understanding later Greek spyware and EYP controversies.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Attribution remains disputed; the existence of the interception is documented.
