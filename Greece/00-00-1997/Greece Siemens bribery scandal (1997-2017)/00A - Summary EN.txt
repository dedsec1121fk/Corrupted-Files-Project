Greece: Siemens bribery scandal (1997-2017) 1) One of the classic Greek corruption cases of the modern era Status: [DOCUMENTED / STRONG EVIDENCE] Summary: The Siemens scandal in Greece centered on allegations that bribes were paid to win contracts, especially around the telecommunications sector and public procurement. Reuters reported in 2008 that a Greek prosecutor filed preliminary criminal charges, in 2012 that Greece agreed to a settlement with Siemens worth 270 million euros, and in 2017 that a former Greek minister was found guilty of money laundering linked to contracts between Siemens and OTE.
