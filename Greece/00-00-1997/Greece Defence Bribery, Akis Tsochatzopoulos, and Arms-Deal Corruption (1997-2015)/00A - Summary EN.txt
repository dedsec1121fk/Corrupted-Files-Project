Greece: Defence Bribery, Akis Tsochatzopoulos, and Arms-Deal Corruption (1997-2015) 1) Kickbacks around arms deals became one of modern Greece's most notorious corruption symbols Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: The Akis Tsochatzopoulos case became one of the clearest Greek examples of defence-sector corruption at elite level. Reuters reported that Tsochatzopoulos, who served as defence minister from 1996 to 2001, was convicted of putting kickbacks from arms deals between 1997 and 2001 into foreign bank accounts. The case was widely treated as a symbol of the graft that plagued crisis-era Greece. Reuters also reported in 2015 that Greece sought more than 100 million euros in compensation from German defence firms over alleged bribes connected to helicopters and submarine contracts, and that Greece hoped for much more across a broader series of cases.
