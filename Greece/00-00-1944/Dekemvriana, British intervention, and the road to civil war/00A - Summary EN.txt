Dekemvriana, British intervention, and the road to civil war Country: Greece Year: 1944 Category: postwar state power / foreign influence / political violence Evidence level: documented historical conflict with contested responsibility Full story ---------- The December 1944 clashes in Athens turned liberation from occupation into a struggle over who would control the postwar Greek state. British forces, Greek government units, EAM/ELAS, police structures, and civilians were drawn into a conflict that still shapes Greek memory because it joined anti-fascist resistance, foreign policy, monarchy questions, and fear of communist power.
