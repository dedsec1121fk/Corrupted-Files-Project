Dekemvriana, foreign intervention, state violence and civil-war memory (1944) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 1944 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Policing, force and institutional accountability FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Occupation, resistance, British intervention and rival armed legitimacy created a foundation for later files, exile and civil-war memory. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is coercive power backed by uniforms, emergency language, prison authority, border authority or public-order narratives. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
