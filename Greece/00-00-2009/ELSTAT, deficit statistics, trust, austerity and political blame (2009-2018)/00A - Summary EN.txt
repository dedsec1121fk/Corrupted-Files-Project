ELSTAT, deficit statistics, trust, austerity and political blame (2009-2018) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 2009 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Corruption, capture and public money FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Statistics became sovereign power when deficit numbers shaped bailout terms, austerity narratives and national blame. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is networks of contracts, favors, media protection, political access, weak audits and delayed courts. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
