Greece: The Lagarde List, Tax-Evasion Politics, and Missing Accountability (2010-2015) 1) A list of wealthy Greeks with Swiss accounts became a scandal about state protection and selective enforcement Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: The “Lagarde List” became one of the clearest Greek crisis-era symbols of elite impunity. French authorities provided Greece with a list of 2,059 Greeks holding HSBC accounts in Switzerland for possible tax-evasion checks. Instead of becoming a clean major anti-evasion case, the list turned into a scandal about delay, mishandling, disappearance, media prosecution, and political tampering. Reuters reported that the list was passed from one senior official to another and even got misplaced without apparent action.
