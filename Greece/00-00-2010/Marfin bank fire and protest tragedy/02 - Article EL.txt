Marfin bank fire and protest tragedy
====================================

Χώρα: Ελλάδα
Φάκελος έτους: 2010
Κατηγορία: austerity / political violence
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented deaths with contested responsibility

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Three bank workers died in a fire during an anti-austerity protest in
Athens.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Marfin is one of the most painful austerity-era files because it turned
protest, workplace pressure, policing, arson and media war into one tragedy. It should not
be used to erase peaceful protest or to excuse violence. It should be read as a warning
about how mass anger can create conditions where trapped workers pay the price.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2010: fire during protest.
- Afterward: trials, accusations and memorial conflict.
- Long-term: Marfin becomes shorthand for protest tragedy.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Three employees died.
- The event occurred during mass anti-austerity demonstrations.
- The case remains politically charged.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It shows the human cost when political conflict loses boundaries.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Legal and factual responsibility has been contested; the deaths and public shock
are documented.

Πηγές / διαδρομή επαλήθευσης
