Marfin bank fire and protest tragedy ==================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 2010 Κατηγορία: austerity / political violence Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented deaths with contested responsibility Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Three bank workers died in a fire during an anti-austerity protest in Athens. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Marfin is one of the most painful austerity-era files because it turned protest, workplace pressure, policing, arson and media war into one tragedy. It should not be used to erase peaceful protest or to excuse violence. It should be read as a warning about how mass anger can create conditions where trapped workers pay the price.
