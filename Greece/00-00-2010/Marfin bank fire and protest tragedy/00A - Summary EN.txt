Marfin bank fire and protest tragedy ==================================== Country: Greece Year folder: 2010 Category: austerity / political violence Evidence level: documented deaths with contested responsibility Core story ---------- Three bank workers died in a fire during an anti-austerity protest in Athens. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ Marfin is one of the most painful austerity-era files because it turned protest, workplace pressure, policing, arson and media war into one tragedy. It should not be used to erase peaceful protest or to excuse violence. It should be read as a warning about how mass anger can create conditions where trapped workers pay the price.
