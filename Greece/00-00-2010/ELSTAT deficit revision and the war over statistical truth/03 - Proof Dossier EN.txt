ELSTAT deficit revision and the war over statistical truth — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Greek public-finance statistics were under European scrutiny before and during the crisis.
- The revised deficit figures became central to bailout politics.
- Legal disputes and European statistical-institution statements followed for years.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- The line between technical correction and political responsibility remains contested.
- Accusations against statisticians and politicians often mix evidence with crisis anger.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- One theory says Greece was deliberately framed by inflated numbers; another says old governments hid the truth until collapse. Both require document discipline.
- The structural reading is that statistical independence becomes explosive when numbers decide sovereignty.

Source trail
------------
- Eurostat and ELSTAT public record
- Greek court reporting
- European statistical governance materials

Evidence discipline: prefer court records, official inquiries, archival files, regulator findings, named investigative reporting, and primary documents over anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.
