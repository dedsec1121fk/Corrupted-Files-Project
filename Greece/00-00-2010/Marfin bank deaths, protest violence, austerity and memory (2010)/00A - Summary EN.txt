Marfin bank deaths, protest violence, austerity and memory (2010) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 2010 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Policing, force and institutional accountability FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Three workers died during protest violence, creating one of the darkest moral files of the Greek austerity crisis. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is coercive power backed by uniforms, emergency language, prison authority, border authority or public-order narratives. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
