Structured bonds, pension funds, and public-money opacity — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The scandal involved Greek pension funds and structured financial products.
- Public debate focused on intermediaries, pricing, commissions, and state oversight.
- The case damaged trust in pension-fund governance before the debt crisis.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Legal responsibility depends on documents, pricing models, approvals, and knowledge of risk.
- Not every complex product is corrupt; opacity and incentives are the key questions.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The theory lane says pension funds were used as a dumping ground for connected financial deals; that claim must be tested fund by fund.
- A structural reading is that complexity transfers power from beneficiaries to intermediaries.

Evidence discipline
-------------------
Use primary records, official investigations, court files, regulator documents, and named reporting before anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.

Source trail
------------
- Greek parliamentary and judicial material on structured bonds
- Financial press archives
- Pension-fund governance reports
