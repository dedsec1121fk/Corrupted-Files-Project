C4I Olympic security system scandal
===================================

Χώρα: Ελλάδα
Φάκελος έτους: 2004
Κατηγορία: procurement / surveillance infrastructure
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented procurement controversy

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Greece’s Olympic security procurement became a long-running controversy
over cost, delivery and surveillance infrastructure.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The 2004 Olympics created genuine security pressure. That pressure justified
large systems, foreign contractors and urgent procurement. The C4I controversy shows how
mega-events can become laboratories for surveillance technology and public spending with
weak accountability.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2004: Olympics held in Athens.
- Afterward: procurement questions continue.
- Later years: investigations and political blame.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Olympics created exceptional security demands.
- C4I became tied to corruption and delivery questions.
- The case intersects with Siemens-era procurement concerns.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It connects security panic, contracts and post-Olympic public debt culture.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Some systems may have had legitimate security value; the scandal concerns
procurement, cost, delivery and transparency.

Πηγές / διαδρομή επαλήθευσης
----------------------------
- European Parliament documents: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/
