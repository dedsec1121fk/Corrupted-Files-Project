Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The 2004 Olympics created genuine security pressure. That pressure justified large systems, foreign contractors and urgent procurement. The C4I controversy shows how mega-events can become laboratories for surveillance technology and public spending with weak accountability.
