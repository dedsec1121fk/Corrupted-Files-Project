Greece: Debt Crisis, Misreported Fiscal Data, and the Austerity Era (2004-2018) 1) Statistical misreporting was real; the social cost of the correction was historic Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: The Greek debt crisis was not just a market panic. It was also a governance and statistics crisis. Eurostat's 2010 report says there had been Greek misreporting of Excessive Deficit Procedure data in 2004 and discusses the weakness of the controls available to Eurostat. A European Parliament briefing from 2010 says the Papandreou government confirmed that previous Greek fiscal data had been misreported and that the budget deficit figures were far worse than previously presented.
