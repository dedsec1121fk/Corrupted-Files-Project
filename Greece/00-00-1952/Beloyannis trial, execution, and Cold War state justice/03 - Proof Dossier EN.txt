Beloyannis trial, execution, and Cold War state justice — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The trial and execution are part of the Greek post-civil-war historical record.
- International appeals and public campaigns made the case visible beyond Greece.
- The case remains a reference point for political justice and emergency law.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Supporters and critics still disagree over espionage, political repression, and military-court legitimacy.
- The archival record should be separated from later party mythology.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says the execution was demanded by foreign Cold War managers; foreign pressure and context matter, but a direct command claim needs documents.
- A structural reading is that anti-communist state building made mercy politically impossible.
