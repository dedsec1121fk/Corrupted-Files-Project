Greek occupation famine and collaboration networks ================================================== Country: Greece Year folder: 1941 Category: war / survival / collaboration Evidence level: documented humanitarian catastrophe Core story ---------- Occupation Greece suffered famine, black market profiteering, resistance, collaboration and mass trauma. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The famine was not only natural scarcity. It involved occupation extraction, transport collapse, blockade conditions, black-market networks and state breakdown. In this environment, some people resisted, some survived through compromise, and some profited. The case shows how social morality changes under hunger.
