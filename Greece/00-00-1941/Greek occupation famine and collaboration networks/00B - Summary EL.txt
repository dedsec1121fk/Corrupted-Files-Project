Greek occupation famine and collaboration networks ================================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1941 Κατηγορία: war / survival / collaboration Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented humanitarian catastrophe Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Occupation Greece suffered famine, black market profiteering, resistance, collaboration and mass trauma. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The famine was not only natural scarcity. It involved occupation extraction, transport collapse, blockade conditions, black-market networks and state breakdown. In this environment, some people resisted, some survived through compromise, and some profited. The case shows how social morality changes under hunger.
