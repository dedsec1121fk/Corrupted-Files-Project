Cyprus coup, junta responsibility, invasion and national trauma (1974) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 1974 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Surveillance, secrecy and information control FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER The junta-backed coup against Makarios helped trigger invasion and partition, showing catastrophic foreign-policy failure under dictatorship. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is secrecy plus asymmetry: one side collects, classifies, stores or shapes information while ordinary people cannot see the full system. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
