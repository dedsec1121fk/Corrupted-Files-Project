Greece: Mati wildfire, state failure, and accountability limits (2018-2025) 1) A deadly disaster that became a test of whether the state could hold itself accountable Status: [DOCUMENTED / STRONG EVIDENCE] Summary: The 2018 Mati wildfire killed 104 people and became a symbol of state failure in emergency response. Reuters and AP reported that courts eventually convicted officials and an arson suspect over negligence and mishandling, but many families remained outraged by acquittals, misdemeanor treatment, or suspended and limited punishments. Why it matters: - It shows how disasters become scandals when the public believes state systems failed before, during, and after the event. - It demonstrates that accountability can exist on paper yet still feel inadequate in practice. - It also matters because the tragedy damaged public trust across party lines.
