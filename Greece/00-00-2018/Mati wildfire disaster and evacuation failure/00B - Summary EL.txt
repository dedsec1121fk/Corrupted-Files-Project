Mati wildfire disaster and evacuation failure ============================================= Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 2018 Κατηγορία: disaster governance / accountability Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented disaster Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The Mati wildfire killed more than 100 people and exposed failures in warning, evacuation and emergency coordination. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Mati is one of Greece’s most devastating modern disaster files. The fire moved fast, but the human questions focus on planning, alerts, road design, illegal construction, emergency coordination and political communication. Disasters become corruption files when preventable vulnerabilities are ignored for years.
