Mati wildfire, evacuation failure, and command accountability — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The death toll and scale of destruction are documented.
- Official inquiries and trials examined failures in evacuation, coordination, and command.
- The settlement pattern around Mati made escape routes difficult, but emergency decisions still mattered.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Trials and appeals continue to shape the legal meaning of negligence and duty.
- Public anger also targets political communication during and after the fire, which is harder to measure legally.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The harshest theory says officials knowingly minimized the disaster for political optics during the first hours; intent is harder to prove than bad information flow and delayed acknowledgment.
- Other theories blame arson networks or land interests; ignition questions should not distract from documented civil-protection failures.

Source trail
------------
- Greek court proceedings on the Mati fire
- Official fire-service and civil-protection record
- Survivor testimony and investigative reporting

Evidence discipline: court findings, official reports, regulatory records, parliamentary files, and named investigative reporting are stronger than rumor, anonymous posts, or claims that require a hidden master plan.
