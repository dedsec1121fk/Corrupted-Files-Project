Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case forces a difficult question: how many people can participate in dehumanization before death becomes possible? It involved public violence, policing, media framing and stigma against queer, HIV-positive and activist identities.
