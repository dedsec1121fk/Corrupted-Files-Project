Apostasia crisis, palace pressure, and parliamentary defections Country: Greece Year: 1965 Category: constitutional crisis / palace influence Evidence level: documented political crisis with disputed motives Full story ---------- The 1965 Apostasia crisis broke public confidence in parliamentary democracy before the dictatorship. The fight over royal power, party discipline, defections, intelligence suspicion, and control of the military turned ordinary government formation into a legitimacy crisis. Confirmed record ---------------- - The resignation of Georgios Papandreou and subsequent governments formed with defectors are documented political events. - The palace played an active constitutional and political role. - The crisis deepened polarization and distrust before the 1967 coup.
