ASPIDA affair, army politics, and palace pressure — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The affair entered the public record during the 1960s constitutional crisis.
- It was tied to allegations about officers, political influence, and control of the army.
- The case intensified distrust before the 1967 dictatorship.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- The existence, scale, and intent of any organized officer plot remain disputed.
- Claims about palace, intelligence, and foreign exploitation must be separated from documented proceedings.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says ASPIDA was inflated to break civilian control and weaken the Papandreou camp; the pressure pattern is plausible, but each actor's role needs documents.
- The structural reading is that an army with political veto power makes democracy conditional.

Evidence discipline
-------------------
Use primary records, official investigations, court files, regulator documents, and named reporting before anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.

Source trail
------------
- Greek parliamentary and court record on ASPIDA
- Historical studies of the 1965 crisis
- Contemporaneous Greek press archives
