Greece bankruptcy and Trikoupis debt crisis =========================================== Country: Greece Year folder: 1893 Category: public finance / sovereignty Evidence level: documented historical crisis Core story ---------- Greece’s 1893 bankruptcy became a foundational memory of debt, foreign creditors and political limits. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The case matters because Greek public life repeatedly returns to the same triangle: borrowing, modernization promises and external supervision. Trikoupis did not simply announce a number; he announced a national limitation. Later generations remembered the phrase as evidence that economics can become a sovereignty crisis.
