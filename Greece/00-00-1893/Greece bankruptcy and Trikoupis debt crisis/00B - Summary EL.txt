Greece bankruptcy and Trikoupis debt crisis =========================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1893 Κατηγορία: public finance / sovereignty Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented historical crisis Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Greece’s 1893 bankruptcy became a foundational memory of debt, foreign creditors and political limits. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case matters because Greek public life repeatedly returns to the same triangle: borrowing, modernization promises and external supervision. Trikoupis did not simply announce a number; he announced a national limitation. Later generations remembered the phrase as evidence that economics can become a sovereignty crisis.
