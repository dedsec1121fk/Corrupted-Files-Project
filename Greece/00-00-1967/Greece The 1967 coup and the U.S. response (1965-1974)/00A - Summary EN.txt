Greece: The 1967 coup and the U.S. response (1965-1974) 1) Breakdown of constitutional order, then a military regime Status: [DOCUMENTED] Summary: The 1967 military coup in Greece is not a conspiracy theory. It is a documented collapse of constitutional government followed by dictatorship. What remains debated is not whether the coup happened, but the exact degree and form of U.S. foreknowledge, tolerance, or influence. The official U.S. Foreign Relations of the United States volumes now openly host documentary sections on the breakdown of constitutional government in Greece and "The Military Coup in Greece and the Initial U.S. Response."
