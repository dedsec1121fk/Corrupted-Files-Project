Core story
----------
The 1967 military coup suspended democracy and installed a dictatorship that ruled until
1974.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
The junta case must be read domestically and geopolitically. Greek officers acted inside a
Cold War world where anti-communism could excuse repression. Torture, exile, censorship and
propaganda were not side issues; they were the operating system of the regime.

Timeline
--------

- 1973: Polytechnic uprising.
- 1974: Cyprus crisis and fall of junta.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------

- Political freedoms were suspended.
- The regime collapsed after the Cyprus catastrophe.
