Junta coup and NATO era anti-communist state ============================================ Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1967 Κατηγορία: dictatorship / foreign context Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented coup Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The 1967 military coup suspended democracy and installed a dictatorship that ruled until 1974. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The junta case must be read domestically and geopolitically. Greek officers acted inside a Cold War world where anti-communism could excuse repression. Torture, exile, censorship and propaganda were not side issues; they were the operating system of the regime. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - 1967: coup. - 1973: Polytechnic uprising. - 1974: Cyprus crisis and fall of junta.
