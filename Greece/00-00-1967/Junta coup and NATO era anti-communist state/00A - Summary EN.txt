Junta coup and NATO era anti-communist state ============================================ Country: Greece Year folder: 1967 Category: dictatorship / foreign context Evidence level: documented coup Core story ---------- The 1967 military coup suspended democracy and installed a dictatorship that ruled until 1974. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The junta case must be read domestically and geopolitically. Greek officers acted inside a Cold War world where anti-communism could excuse repression. Torture, exile, censorship and propaganda were not side issues; they were the operating system of the regime. Timeline -------- - 1967: coup. - 1973: Polytechnic uprising. - 1974: Cyprus crisis and fall of junta.
