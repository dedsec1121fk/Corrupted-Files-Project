Junta coup and NATO era anti-communist state
============================================

Χώρα: Ελλάδα
Φάκελος έτους: 1967
Κατηγορία: dictatorship / foreign context
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented coup

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The 1967 military coup suspended democracy and installed a dictatorship
that ruled until 1974.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The junta case must be read domestically and geopolitically. Greek officers
acted inside a Cold War world where anti-communism could excuse repression. Torture, exile,
censorship and propaganda were not side issues; they were the operating system of the
regime.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1967: coup.
- 1973: Polytechnic uprising.
- 1974: Cyprus crisis and fall of junta.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Coup occurred on 21 April 1967.
- Political freedoms were suspended.
- The regime collapsed after the Cyprus catastrophe.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is the master file for modern Greek fear of surveillance, police files and
foreign-backed authoritarianism.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: The exact foreign role is debated in parts, but the dictatorship and its repression
are documented.
