Pispirigou child deaths media trial problem =========================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 2022 Κατηγορία: crime / media ethics Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented criminal case with media controversy Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The deaths of children in Patras and the prosecution of Roula Pispirigou became a massive Greek true-crime media event. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This case belongs in the database not because it is state corruption but because it shows a different corruption: the corruption of public attention. Grief, suspicion, forensic claims, television panels and social-media certainty turned a criminal process into entertainment.
