Violence and fraud election, parastate networks and democratic trust (1961) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 1961 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Surveillance, secrecy and information control FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER The 1961 election became shorthand for intimidation, administrative manipulation and fear that formal democracy could be substantively distorted. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is secrecy plus asymmetry: one side collects, classifies, stores or shapes information while ordinary people cannot see the full system. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
