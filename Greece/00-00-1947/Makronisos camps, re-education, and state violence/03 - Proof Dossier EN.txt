Makronisos camps, re-education, and state violence — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Makronisos operated as a detention and re-education site in the postwar period.
- Former detainee testimony and historical research document coercion and abuse.
- The island remains a major memory site for civil-war state violence.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Exact chains of command and responsibility vary across periods and units.
- Later party memory can simplify the range of experiences inside the camp system.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says the camps were a foreign-designed laboratory for anti-communist social engineering; foreign Cold War context matters, but direct design claims need documents.
- The structural reading is that emergency politics made coercive loyalty production part of state rebuilding.

Source trail
------------
- Greek civil-war historical archives
- Former detainee testimony collections
- Academic studies of Makronisos
