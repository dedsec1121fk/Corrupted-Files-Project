Grigoropoulos killing and December revolt ========================================= Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 2008 Κατηγορία: policing / youth uprising Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented killing and unrest Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The police killing of Alexandros Grigoropoulos triggered nationwide unrest and became a generational rupture. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The shooting converted accumulated youth anger, economic insecurity and police distrust into street revolt. It was not only about one officer; it was about whether young people believed the state saw them as citizens or enemies. Χρονολόγιο ---------- - Dec 2008: shooting. - Dec 2008: nationwide unrest. - Afterward: trials and long-term political memory.
