Grigoropoulos killing and December revolt ========================================= Country: Greece Year folder: 2008 Category: policing / youth uprising Evidence level: documented killing and unrest Core story ---------- The police killing of Alexandros Grigoropoulos triggered nationwide unrest and became a generational rupture. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The shooting converted accumulated youth anger, economic insecurity and police distrust into street revolt. It was not only about one officer; it was about whether young people believed the state saw them as citizens or enemies. Timeline -------- - Dec 2008: shooting. - Dec 2008: nationwide unrest. - Afterward: trials and long-term political memory.
