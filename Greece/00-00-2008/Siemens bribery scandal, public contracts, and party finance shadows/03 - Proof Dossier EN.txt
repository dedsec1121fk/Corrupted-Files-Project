Siemens bribery scandal, public contracts, and party finance shadows — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Siemens admitted broad bribery practices internationally in settlements outside Greece.
- Greek investigations examined contracts, intermediaries, and political-money allegations.
- The scandal became a symbol of procurement opacity and delayed accountability.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- The exact map of political beneficiaries remains contested and unevenly documented.
- Some accountability happened through trials and settlements, but public trust did not recover at the same speed.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy version says foreign vendors bought Greek state policy wholesale; the record supports bribery and influence, while total control claims need evidence.
- A stronger structural claim is that complex procurement and party finance created ideal conditions for hidden payments.

Source trail
------------
- Siemens international settlement record
- Greek parliamentary and judicial material
- Public procurement and telecom contract reporting

Evidence discipline: court findings, official reports, regulatory records, parliamentary files, and named investigative reporting are stronger than rumor, anonymous posts, or claims that require a hidden master plan.
