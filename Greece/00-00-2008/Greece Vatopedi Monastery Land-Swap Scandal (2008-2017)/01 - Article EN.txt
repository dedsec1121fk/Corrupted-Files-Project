Summary:
The Vatopedi scandal centered on land swaps between the Greek state and the Vatopedi Monastery on Mount Athos. Reuters reported in 2008 that preliminary probes found the monastery received prime state-owned real estate in exchange for cheap land, with media estimating heavy losses in land value for the state. The affair became one of the defining pre-crisis corruption symbols in Greece.
