The story On 6 December 2008, 15-year-old Alexandros Grigoropoulos was shot dead by a police officer in Exarchia. The killing triggered riots and protests across Greece. The event became more than one fatal shooting: it symbolized youth anger, police impunity, economic despair, and a generation that felt the state saw it as a threat before it saw it as citizens. 2.
