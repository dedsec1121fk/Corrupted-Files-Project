Michalis Kaltezas killing, police impunity, and youth revolt Country: Greece Year: 1985 Category: police violence / youth protest / accountability Evidence level: documented police killing with contested accountability Full story ---------- The 1985 killing of teenager Michalis Kaltezas by a police officer became a Greek memory file about police violence, Exarchia, youth anger, and whether the justice system treats uniformed violence as ordinary crime or as an exception to be managed politically. Confirmed record ---------------- - Kaltezas was shot after a Polytechnic anniversary demonstration. - The case triggered unrest and became a landmark in Greek police-accountability memory. - Legal proceedings and public debate followed the killing.
