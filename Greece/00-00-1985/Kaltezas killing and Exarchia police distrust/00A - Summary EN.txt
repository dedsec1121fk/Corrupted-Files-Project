Kaltezas killing and Exarchia police distrust ============================================= Country: Greece Year folder: 1985 Category: policing / youth unrest Evidence level: documented killing and social rupture Core story ---------- The police killing of 15-year-old Michalis Kaltezas became a landmark of Greek youth-police distrust. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The case is a pre-2008 warning file. It showed that one killing could turn neighborhood anger into political memory. Exarchia’s later symbolism did not appear from nowhere; it grew from repeated confrontations, policing practices and stories of impunity. Timeline -------- - 1985: killing occurs. - Afterward: protests and trial controversy. - Long-term: Exarchia becomes a symbol of state-youth conflict.
