Kaltezas killing and Exarchia police distrust ============================================= Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1985 Κατηγορία: policing / youth unrest Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented killing and social rupture Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The police killing of 15-year-old Michalis Kaltezas became a landmark of Greek youth-police distrust. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case is a pre-2008 warning file. It showed that one killing could turn neighborhood anger into political memory. Exarchia’s later symbolism did not appear from nowhere; it grew from repeated confrontations, policing practices and stories of impunity.
