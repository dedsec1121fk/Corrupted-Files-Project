Giorgos Karaivaz assassination and press-freedom wound Country: Greece Year: 2021 Category: journalist killing / organized crime / impunity Evidence level: documented murder; accountability disputed Full story ---------- This dossier records the case as a public-trust event rather than as a short headline. The point is to make the reader understand what happened, why people still discuss it, what can be proven, what remains disputed, and how the case fits into a wider pattern of corruption, state failure, institutional secrecy, organized influence, or public fear. Background ---------- Veteran Greek crime reporter Giorgos Karaivaz was killed outside his home in Alimos on 9 April 2021, turning a targeted shooting into a national press-freedom case.
