Country: Greece

Full story

Background
----------
Veteran Greek crime reporter Giorgos Karaivaz was killed outside his home in Alimos on 9 April 2021, turning a targeted shooting into a national press-freedom case.

Chronology and public record
----------------------------
Two suspects were tried and acquitted in July 2024 after the court found guilt had not been proven beyond reasonable doubt, while press-freedom groups described the outcome as another sign of impunity.

Main conflict
-------------
The conflict is between the fact of a public assassination and the difficulty of proving the chain of planning, motive, and responsibility behind it.

Why it matters
--------------
The case matters because killing a crime reporter sends a message beyond one person: it pressures journalists, sources, witnesses, and citizens who expect media to expose criminal networks.

Impact on public trust
----------------------
Public trust was damaged because the state was judged not only by arrests, but by whether it could reveal who ordered, enabled, or benefited from the killing.

Open questions
--------------
Open questions include whether the full network behind the murder was ever mapped, whether organized-crime reporting created motive, and whether future evidence can reopen accountability.
