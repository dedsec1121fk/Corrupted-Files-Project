GREECE: GIORGOS KARAIVAZ, JOURNALIST MURDER, AND THE IMPUNITY PROBLEM (2021-2026) Classification: [STRONG EVIDENCE] Why this file exists: When a crime reporter is murdered and nobody is ultimately held responsible, the issue becomes larger than one case. It becomes a test of whether organized crime, political influence, and state weakness overlap. What is documented: - Reuters reported that Karaivaz, a veteran crime journalist, was shot dead near his Athens home in April 2021 in what looked like a professional hit. - Reuters reported in July 2024 that the two men charged over the killing were acquitted for lack of evidence. - CPJ reported in 2025 that an Athens court ruled in December 2024 that Karaivaz was murdered because of his journalistic work, while still leaving the killing unsolved. - CPJ said total impunity persists and that nobody has been held responsible for the murder....
