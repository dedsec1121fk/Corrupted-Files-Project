Evia wildfires, state response, climate failure and rural abandonment (2021) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 2021 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Corruption, capture and public money FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Villages, forests and livelihoods burned while prevention, evacuation and rural protection became the central accountability questions. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is networks of contracts, favors, media protection, political access, weak audits and delayed courts. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
