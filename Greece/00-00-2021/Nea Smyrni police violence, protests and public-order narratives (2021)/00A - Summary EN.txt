Nea Smyrni police violence, protests and public-order narratives (2021) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 2021 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Policing, force and institutional accountability FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER During pandemic restrictions, one video helped turn a local police incident into national anger about humiliation and force. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is coercive power backed by uniforms, emergency language, prison authority, border authority or public-order narratives. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
