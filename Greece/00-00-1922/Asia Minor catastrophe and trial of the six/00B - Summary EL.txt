Asia Minor catastrophe and trial of the six =========================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1922 Κατηγορία: war disaster / accountability Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented national trauma Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The defeat in Asia Minor, refugee catastrophe and execution of political- military leaders shaped modern Greek memory. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This file is not about blaming refugees or soldiers. It is about how nationalist ambition, alliance miscalculation, military overstretch and political division produced catastrophe. The Trial of the Six tried to answer public fury quickly, but speed and trauma make accountability complicated.
