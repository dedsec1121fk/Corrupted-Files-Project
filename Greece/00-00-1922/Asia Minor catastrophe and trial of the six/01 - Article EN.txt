Asia Minor catastrophe and trial of the six
===========================================

Country: Greece
Year folder: 1922
Category: war disaster / accountability

Core story
----------
The defeat in Asia Minor, refugee catastrophe and execution of political-military leaders
shaped modern Greek memory.

What happened, in full context
------------------------------
This file is not about blaming refugees or soldiers. It is about how nationalist ambition,
alliance miscalculation, military overstretch and political division produced catastrophe.
The Trial of the Six tried to answer public fury quickly, but speed and trauma make
accountability complicated.

Timeline
--------
- 1922: front collapses and Smyrna catastrophe.
- 1922: trial and executions.
- Aftermath: refugee settlement reshapes Greece.

Key facts and evidence points
-----------------------------
- Mass displacement transformed Greece.
- Political and military leaders were executed after trial.
- The event shaped Greek-Turkish relations and domestic identity.

Why this case matters
---------------------
It is a central Greek file on how national myths, elite decisions and human displacement
collide.

Competing explanations and caution
-----------------------------------
Responsibility is historically debated; the refugee suffering and state shock are beyond
dispute.
