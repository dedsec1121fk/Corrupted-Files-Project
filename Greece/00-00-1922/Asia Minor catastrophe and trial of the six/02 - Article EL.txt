Asia Minor catastrophe and trial of the six
===========================================

Χώρα: Ελλάδα
Φάκελος έτους: 1922
Κατηγορία: war disaster / accountability
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented national trauma

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: The defeat in Asia Minor, refugee catastrophe and execution of political-
military leaders shaped modern Greek memory.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This file is not about blaming refugees or soldiers. It is about how
nationalist ambition, alliance miscalculation, military overstretch and political division
produced catastrophe. The Trial of the Six tried to answer public fury quickly, but speed
and trauma make accountability complicated.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 1922: front collapses and Smyrna catastrophe.
- 1922: trial and executions.
- Aftermath: refugee settlement reshapes Greece.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Mass displacement transformed Greece.
- Political and military leaders were executed after trial.
- The event shaped Greek-Turkish relations and domestic identity.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It is a central Greek file on how national myths, elite decisions and human
displacement collide.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Responsibility is historically debated; the refugee suffering and state shock are
beyond dispute.
