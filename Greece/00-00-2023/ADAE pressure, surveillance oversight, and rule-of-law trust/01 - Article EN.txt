ADAE pressure, surveillance oversight, and rule-of-law trust

Country: Greece
Year: 2023
Category: surveillance oversight / independent authorities
Evidence level: documented public controversy around oversight and institutional pressure

Full story
----------
After Greece's surveillance scandals, oversight itself became a battlefield. ADAE, prosecutors, parliament, telecom providers, privacy law, and intelligence secrecy collided over whether independent authorities could verify who was watched and why.

Confirmed record
----------------
- Greece had major public controversy over lawful interception and spyware allegations.
- ADAE and other institutions became central to oversight disputes.
- European rule-of-law criticism repeatedly referenced surveillance and accountability.

Disputed or unresolved points
-----------------------------
- Legal secrecy, national-security claims, and privacy rights collide in each file.
- Attribution between state, private spyware actors, and intermediaries remains contested in parts.

Conspiracy theory / rumor lane
------------------------------

- The structural reading is that secrecy can turn oversight into theatre if authorities cannot access evidence.
