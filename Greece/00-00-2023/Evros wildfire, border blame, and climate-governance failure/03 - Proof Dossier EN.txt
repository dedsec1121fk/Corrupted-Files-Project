Evros wildfire, border blame, and climate-governance failure — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The Evros fire was one of the major Greek wildfire disasters of 2023.
- The disaster occurred in a border region already shaped by migration and security narratives.
- Public debate included prevention, evacuation, biodiversity, and blame claims.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Specific ignition causes need forensic findings, not social-media blame.
- The balance between extreme weather and institutional preparedness remains contested.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- Some theories blamed migrants as a group; such claims can become political weaponry unless tied to specific evidence.
- A structural reading is that climate disasters expose systems already weakened by security-first thinking.

Evidence discipline
-------------------
Use primary records, official investigations, court files, regulator documents, and named reporting before anonymous claims or broad hidden-plan narratives.

Source trail
------------
- Greek civil-protection and fire-service public record
- EU wildfire monitoring materials
- Environmental and human-rights reporting on Evros
