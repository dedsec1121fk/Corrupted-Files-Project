ARTICLE The Tempi train crash is not only a transport disaster. It is a national trust disaster. On February 28, 2023, a passenger train carrying many students collided with a freight train near Tempi in central Greece. Fifty-seven people died. The collision and fireball exposed years of railway safety problems, delayed systems, administrative negligence, and public anger over whether the state had ignored warnings until death made them impossible to deny. A disaster file must begin with the victims. They were not statistics, political symbols, or hashtags. They were people traveling through a system they had every right to assume was safe.
