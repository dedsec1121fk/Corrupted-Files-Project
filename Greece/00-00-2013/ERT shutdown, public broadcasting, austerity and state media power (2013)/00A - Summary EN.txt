ERT shutdown, public broadcasting, austerity and state media power (2013) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 2013 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Surveillance, secrecy and information control FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER A public broadcaster was abruptly closed in the name of austerity, raising questions about state power over public information. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is secrecy plus asymmetry: one side collects, classifies, stores or shapes information while ordinary people cannot see the full system. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
