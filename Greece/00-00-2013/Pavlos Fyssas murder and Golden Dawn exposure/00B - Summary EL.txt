Pavlos Fyssas murder and Golden Dawn exposure ============================================= Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 2013 Κατηγορία: political violence / extremism Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented murder and criminal organization case Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The murder of musician Pavlos Fyssas accelerated the exposure and prosecution of Golden Dawn. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: Fyssas’s killing made visible what many victims and activists had warned about: organized far-right violence was not random street conflict. The later trial and conviction of Golden Dawn as a criminal organization became a rare accountability moment against extremist political machinery.
