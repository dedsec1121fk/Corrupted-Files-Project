Metaxas dictatorship and censorship machine =========================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1936 Κατηγορία: authoritarianism / censorship Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented dictatorship Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: The 4th of August regime suspended democratic life, censored culture and promoted an authoritarian national project. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The regime used anti-communism, royal backing, youth mobilization, censorship and police files to build obedience. Its importance for this database is that authoritarianism did not arrive only through foreign occupation; it grew from internal crisis, fear and elite acceptance.
