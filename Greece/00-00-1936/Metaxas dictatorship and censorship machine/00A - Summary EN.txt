Metaxas dictatorship and censorship machine =========================================== Country: Greece Year folder: 1936 Category: authoritarianism / censorship Evidence level: documented dictatorship Core story ---------- The 4th of August regime suspended democratic life, censored culture and promoted an authoritarian national project. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ The regime used anti-communism, royal backing, youth mobilization, censorship and police files to build obedience. Its importance for this database is that authoritarianism did not arrive only through foreign occupation; it grew from internal crisis, fear and elite acceptance. Timeline -------- - 1936: dictatorship established. - 1936-1941: censorship and repression. - 1940: war with Italy changes memory of regime.
