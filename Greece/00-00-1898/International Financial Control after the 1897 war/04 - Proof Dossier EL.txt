Πηγές:
Greek public debt histories; international financial-control records.

Πρώτο γεγονός προς επαλήθευση:
The postwar debt-control regime limited Greek fiscal autonomy after defeat and bankruptcy pressure.

Θεσμικό ερώτημα:
It matters because corruption is not only bribery; it can also mean democratic choices narrowed by debt machinery and creditor oversight.

Ανοιχτό/αμφισβητούμενο σημείο:
The facts of control are documented; interpretations differ on necessity versus humiliation.
