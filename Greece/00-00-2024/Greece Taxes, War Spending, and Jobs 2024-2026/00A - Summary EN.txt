Greece Taxes, War Spending, and Jobs: 2024-2026 Why this file exists: In Greece, distrust is often not abstract. People ask whether taxes, austerity habits, defence burdens, EU/NATO obligations, and weak job quality are all part of the same pressure system. This file is not a claim that every euro is stolen. It is a record of tax burden, defence prioritization, labor stress, and the policy response. 1) Greece remains a relatively heavy-tax labour economy. The OECD says the tax wedge for the average single worker in Greece was 39.3% in 2024, up from 38.7% in 2023 and above the OECD average of 34.9%. That means the labour tax burden on a standard worker remains high by OECD standards.
