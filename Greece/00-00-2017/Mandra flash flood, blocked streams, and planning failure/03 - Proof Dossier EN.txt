Mandra flash flood, blocked streams, and planning failure — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Twenty-four people died in the disaster.
- Investigations and court proceedings examined local, regional, and planning responsibilities.
- The public record repeatedly focused on flood channels, unauthorized structures, and delayed public works.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Responsibility is distributed across decades, administrations, property choices, and emergency response decisions.
- The exact share of fault between natural intensity and institutional neglect remains debated.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- Rumors claim protected construction interests were deliberately shielded for votes and money; some local incentives are credible, but a single master plan is not proven.
- The stronger reading is systemic tolerance: many small permissions and delays created one large disaster.

Source trail
------------
- Greek judicial proceedings on the Mandra flood
- Civil protection and regional planning record
- Contemporaneous Greek reporting

Evidence discipline: court findings, official reports, regulatory records, parliamentary files, and named investigative reporting are stronger than rumor, anonymous posts, or claims that require a hidden master plan.
