Mandra flash flood, blocked streams, and planning failure Country: Greece Year: 2017 Category: disaster / urban planning / accountability Evidence level: documented disaster with official and judicial review Full story ---------- The 2017 Mandra flood killed residents and exposed how illegal construction, blocked streams, weak drainage, and delayed flood works can turn weather into an accountability case. The scandal was not that rain fell; it was that known vulnerabilities met a state that had normalized risk. Confirmed record ---------------- - Twenty-four people died in the disaster. - Investigations and court proceedings examined local, regional, and planning responsibilities. - The public record repeatedly focused on flood channels, unauthorized structures, and delayed public works.
