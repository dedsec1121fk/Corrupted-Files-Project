Koskotas, Bank of Crete, media money and political power (1988-1992) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 1989 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Corruption, capture and public money FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER The scandal became a Greek template for the triangle of banks, media ownership and political power. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is networks of contracts, favors, media protection, political access, weak audits and delayed courts. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
