Background
----------
The Koskotas scandal connected banking, media power, party politics, and allegations of corruption in late-1980s Greece.

Chronology and public record
----------------------------
The case reshaped the political climate and fed a period of trials, polarization, and institutional suspicion.

Main conflict
-------------
The main conflict was whether media and financial machinery were being used to purchase influence.

Why it matters
--------------
It matters because it showed how press power and banking power can merge into political leverage.

Impact on public trust
----------------------
Public trust suffered because the scandal suggested that money, publicity, and state access could form a closed circuit.
