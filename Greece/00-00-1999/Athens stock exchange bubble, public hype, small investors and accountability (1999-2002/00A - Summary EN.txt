Athens stock exchange bubble, public hype, small investors and accountability (1999-2002) COUNTRY: Greece YEAR FOLDER: 1999 EVIDENCE LEVEL: documented/partly documented public record; disputed parts are labelled as disputed CATEGORY: Corruption, capture and public money FULL ARTICLE / DOSSIER Small investors entered a market shaped by hype and national optimism, then carried losses when paper wealth collapsed. BACKGROUND The deeper mechanism in this type of case is networks of contracts, favors, media protection, political access, weak audits and delayed courts. The visible scandal is usually only the surface. Under it there are incentives, habits, institutions, budgets, files, contracts, social fears and political stories that existed before the public paid attention.
