Private TV licensing, media power, and political dependency — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- Private television expanded rapidly after the late 1980s.
- Licensing and ownership debates remained politically charged for decades.
- Media ownership overlapped with other economic sectors and state-facing interests.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Not every outlet or owner acted the same way.
- Influence often appears through patterns rather than single smoking-gun documents.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- The conspiracy lane says every major newsroom is centrally controlled by hidden owners; the better-supported claim is dependence through licensing, debt, advertising, and state contracts.
- The structural reading is mutual hostage-taking between media, politicians, banks, and public works.
