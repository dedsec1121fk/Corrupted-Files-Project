Temponeras murder and education protest violence ================================================ Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 1991 Κατηγορία: political violence / education Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented killing Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Teacher Nikos Temponeras was killed during school-occupation conflict, exposing violent party-adjacent networks. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: The case matters because it happened around education, not war or espionage. It showed how political polarization can arm local actors and how a school dispute can become deadly. It remains a reference point whenever Greek protests face organized counter- force.
