Halyvourgia strike, riot police intervention, and austerity labor conflict — PROOF DOSSIER

Verified anchors
----------------
- The strike lasted for months during the austerity period.
- Riot police intervened to reopen the plant.
- The dispute became a symbol for labor rights under crisis pressure.

Disputed / weaker evidence
--------------------------
- Economic viability, union strategy, and employer responsibility were argued intensely.
- The legal right to work and the right to strike were framed against each other.

Conspiracy theories to handle carefully
---------------------------------------
- Some theories describe coordinated state-employer strikebreaking; documented police intervention is clear, but coordination claims need records.
- The structural reading is that austerity made police power available to settle economic conflict.
