Public shaming of HIV-positive women ==================================== Χώρα: Ελλάδα Φάκελος έτους: 2012 Κατηγορία: public health / police spectacle Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented rights scandal Κεντρική ιστορία ---------------- Η υπόθεση αφορά: Greek authorities published photos and identities of HIV-positive women accused in a public-health panic. Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο --------------------------- Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This case shows how public health can become police theater. Vulnerable women were treated as threats to respectable society. Later legal and rights criticism exposed the cruelty of using disease, sex-work stigma and media exposure as political proof of toughness.
