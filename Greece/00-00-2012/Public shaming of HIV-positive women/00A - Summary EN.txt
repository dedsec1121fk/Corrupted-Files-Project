Public shaming of HIV-positive women ==================================== Country: Greece Year folder: 2012 Category: public health / police spectacle Evidence level: documented rights scandal Core story ---------- Greek authorities published photos and identities of HIV-positive women accused in a public- health panic. What happened, in full context ------------------------------ This case shows how public health can become police theater. Vulnerable women were treated as threats to respectable society. Later legal and rights criticism exposed the cruelty of using disease, sex-work stigma and media exposure as political proof of toughness.
