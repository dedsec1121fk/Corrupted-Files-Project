Public shaming of HIV-positive women
====================================

Χώρα: Ελλάδα
Φάκελος έτους: 2012
Κατηγορία: public health / police spectacle
Επίπεδο τεκμηρίωσης: documented rights scandal

Κεντρική ιστορία
----------------
Η υπόθεση αφορά: Greek authorities published photos and identities of HIV-positive women
accused in a public-health panic.

Τι συνέβη με πλήρες πλαίσιο
---------------------------
Πλήρες πλαίσιο: This case shows how public health can become police theater. Vulnerable
women were treated as threats to respectable society. Later legal and rights criticism
exposed the cruelty of using disease, sex-work stigma and media exposure as political proof
of toughness.

Χρονολόγιο
----------
- 2012: arrests and public exposure.
- Afterward: legal and rights backlash.
- Long-term: case remains a public-health ethics warning.

Βασικά στοιχεία και σημεία τεκμηρίωσης
--------------------------------------
- Women’s identities and photos were published.
- The case occurred before 2012 elections.
- Rights groups condemned the practice.

Γιατί έχει σημασία
------------------
Σημασία: It belongs in the database because it combines body control, shame and state
publicity.

Αντίθετες εξηγήσεις και προσοχή
-------------------------------
Προσοχή: Authorities framed it as health protection; critics saw rights abuse and
scapegoating.

Πηγές / διαδρομή επαλήθευσης
----------------------------
- European Court of Human Rights HUDOC: https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/
